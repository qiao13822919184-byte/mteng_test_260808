A buyer may place one bulk cutlery order but need stock allocated to several warehouses or store groups. A single total quantity does not tell the supplier which SKU belongs to which destination, or how carton marks should distinguish the groups. Prepare an **allocation sheet** before confirming packing and shipment. It is a planning request, not a promise that the supplier can split production, packing or delivery.

Yumingxing's [buying process](/en/) asks buyers to provide the destination and confirm carton marks, packing details and shipment terms. Its [RFQ guide](/en/blog/what-to-include-in-a-cutlery-rfq/) calls for quantities by component and pack configuration. Those inputs need another dimension when one order has multiple destinations: the quantity assigned to each destination.

## Add destination to the SKU and pack unit

| Allocation column | What to record |
|---|---|
| Destination ID | Buyer-controlled warehouse or store-group name and receiving contact. |
| Product identity | SKU, model and approved revision; keep different set compositions separate. |
| Unit basis | Loose pieces or complete sellable sets, plus pieces per set where relevant. |
| Assigned quantity | Units requested for this destination, with any reserve stock on a separate line. |
| Pack-out request | Units per inner pack and shipping carton; ask whether destination-specific packing is feasible. |
| Mark and handoff | Requested carton identifier, documents and responsible receiving contact. |

For each SKU, **sum of destination assignments + separately identified unallocated reserve = the agreed order quantity**. If it does not reconcile, resolve the difference before approving carton marks. Do not add a line in pieces to a line in sets without first converting them through the approved set composition. The [pieces, sets and cartons guide](/en/blog/flatware-pieces-sets-cartons-order-check/) provides that unit check.

Ask the supplier to confirm whether destination-specific packing or labelling is possible, whether it changes price or minimums, and what shipment handoff is offered. The [retail-box and shipping-carton brief](/en/blog/cutlery-retail-packaging-shipping-carton-brief/) helps keep the consumer unit separate from the transport carton. Send the allocation sheet through the [enquiry page](/en/contact/) with a controlled order revision. If the buyer will split cartons after receiving them at one central warehouse, state that explicitly instead of instructing the supplier to mark multiple final destinations.
