*Research checked on 2026-11-18 for large supermarket procurement teams and e-commerce sales companies. The cover is an AI editorial illustration of generic stainless steel flatware and packaging, not a factory photograph or an identified SKU.*

## 1. Egypt's buying question is configuration before colour

In the Egyptian offers reviewed here, several 24-piece and 30-piece sets both provide six dining settings. The difference is an additional utensil in each setting, not necessarily more people at the table. A buyer who compares only total pieces risks creating a misleading shelf message and an unhelpful online filter.

Our recommendation is to start with an exact utensil list, an Arabic product-information plan and a verified import route. Then test the price and colour variants that the channel can support. The evidence contains three years of reported trade and twelve distinct retail SKUs, but many are related Zinnia variants from one chain. It is an assortment study, not a national demand survey or a ranking of bestselling colours.

## 2. Three import years show recovery below the earlier level

We retrieved UN Comtrade imports for Egypt, reporter 818, world partner 0 and China partner 156, for 2023–2025. The returned classification is H6/HS2022, and primary values match CIF in current USD. HS8215 includes several materials and types of table and kitchen articles, including assorted sets. HS821191 covers fixed-blade table knives. Adding the headings does not produce the stainless steel flatware market. [2023 records](https://comtradeapi.un.org/public/v1/preview/C/A/HS?period=2023&reporterCode=818&cmdCode=8215,821191&flowCode=M&partnerCode=0,156&maxRecords=500), [2024 records](https://comtradeapi.un.org/public/v1/preview/C/A/HS?period=2024&reporterCode=818&cmdCode=8215,821191&flowCode=M&partnerCode=0,156&maxRecords=500), [2025 records](https://comtradeapi.un.org/public/v1/preview/C/A/HS?period=2025&reporterCode=818&cmdCode=8215,821191&flowCode=M&partnerCode=0,156&maxRecords=500).

| 2023 | 7.865 / 5.154 | 65.5% | 0.529 / 0.381 | 72.1% |

HS8215 world imports rose about **7.4%** in 2025 from 2024, while remaining about **27.9%** below 2023. China's share reached **74.9%** in 2025. The separate knife series contracted further. This supports a cautious sourcing comparison, not a claim that all Egyptian cutlery demand is growing at the same rate.

The downloaded records do not provide a usable world net-weight series for HS8215, and some partner weights are estimated. Knife quantity fields are missing or carry a non-usable quantity unit. A zero in such a field is not proof of no physical imports. We do not calculate import unit prices, consumption per household, domestic production or the number of retail sets from these fields. Local retail prices in EGP also cannot be compared directly with CIF USD values without an explicit and dated conversion and cost model.

## 3. National context: distinguish value added, retail sales and internet users

The Ministry of Planning's FY2024/25 GDP note reports **EGP2,382,247.3 million** for wholesale and retail trade at current factor cost. That is sector gross value added, not merchandise sales, e-commerce GMV or cutlery turnover. A verified retail-only national sales total was not established in this review. [Official GDP note, table 2](https://mped.gov.eg/adminpanel/sharedFiles/%5BGDP_Note_Q4_FY24-25%5D_English_version_-_30_Sept._f39.pdf).

MCIT's June 2026 bulletin reports that **50.06% of internet users aged 15–74** made online purchases in the underlying **2022–2023** survey, versus 17.80% in 2019–2020. It also reports cash use among online purchasers at **98.4%**. These are survey-use measures from MCIT/CAPMAS, not 2026 population penetration, transaction-value shares or failed-delivery rates. The publication date must not be substituted for the survey year. [MCIT bulletin, printed page 6](https://mcit.gov.eg/Upcont/Documents/Publications_692026000_ICT_Indicators_Quarterly_Bulletin_Q2_2026.pdf).

For an e-commerce buyer, the useful implication is operational: test payment acceptance, order confirmation and actual collection of proceeds. The survey does not supply a cash-on-delivery cancellation rate to insert into a profit model. For a supermarket, broad sector activity supports researching established retail locations, but says nothing about how fast a particular cutlery colour will sell.

A supplier's annual target cost should not be anchored to a temporary markdown.

## 5. What these price points actually support

The beige 24-piece Zinnia offer at EGP1320 and beige 30-piece offer at EGP1980 both list six complete settings. They imply **EGP220 and EGP330 per setting**, respectively. Their EGP660 ticket difference is not a verified price for the six additional forks: the listings do not establish identical construction, design and finish. This is precisely why a purchasing specification needs more than a count and colour.

Home Centre's 24-piece Elite at EGP1299 is another ticket benchmark, but its unspecified assortment and alloy grade prevent treating it as a like-for-like substitute for Zinnia. Compare the actual handle construction, mass, finish, knife geometry and packaging sample before negotiating a supplier price. No factory cost, gross margin or minimum order can be inferred from either retail ticket.

| Channel | Proposed starting assortment | Evidence needed before expansion |
|---|---|---|
| Large supermarket | A clearly explained six-setting base set; separately identified 30-piece option with the extra utensil shown | Store-week sell-through, shelf ticket response, missing-piece claims and stable replenishment cases |
| E-commerce company | Exact contents and dimensions, colour-specific photography, Arabic information and a tested delivery/payment offer | Delivered contribution, order-confirmation success, payment collection, damage and returns by SKU |
| Both | Limit initial colour duplication; distinguish permanent prices from markdown observations | Independent evidence of demand before allocating deeper stock to a colour |

Tulipe advertises a one-hour delivery option for named areas on its product pages. That is conditional coverage, not a nationwide service promise or an export lead time. A new seller must quote its own verified service area and fulfilment capability, rather than borrowing a competitor's headline. [Example product and coverage](https://tulipe.com/eg_en/zinnia-stainless-steel-30-piece-cutlery-set-j.html).

## 6. The import route and product rules must be addressed together

GOEIC's Decree 43/2016 establishes registration for covered factories and trademark-owning companies supplying goods for trade. Its annex includes **8211.10, 8211.91 and 82.15** within tableware, cutlery and kitchen utensils. The current posted annex retains those headings. Decree 195/2022 amends the process. Confirm the actual exporter, factory or trademark route and documentary validity; a marketplace listing is not registration evidence. [Original decree](https://www.goeic.gov.eg/upload/online/2016/03/documents/files/en/1484.pdf), [current posted annex](https://www.goeic.gov.eg/upload/online/2026/05/documents/files/ar/1852.pdf), [amendment](https://www.goeic.gov.eg/upload/online/2022/03/documents/files/en/1481.pdf).

EOS's current catalogue identifies **ES 494-2/2022** for stainless steel and silver-plated cutlery as obligatory. The original **Decree 561/2022**, article 1 table row 5, lists the standard; EOS's official notification explains the adoption and reference to ISO 8442-2. The public catalogue is not the full paid standard. Obtain the applicable text and agree the laboratory scope for the actual product; we do not invent migration limits, claim automatic acceptance of another certificate or assert that a retailer's “18/10” label demonstrates conformity. [EOS catalogue](https://www.eos.org.eg/en/standard/4449), [original decree](https://www.eos.org.eg/uploads/files/11713_561-2022.PDF), [official adoption notification](https://www.eos.org.eg/ckfinder/userfiles/files/egy281a1.pdf).

Consumer Protection Law 181/2018 articles 3–7 cover conformity, essential product information, readable Arabic consumer documentation and required product data, and clear prices including applicable taxes and financial obligations. Another language may accompany Arabic. Build the local importer identity, product description and supported material claims into the artwork review; the article does not prescribe an unverified universal label template. [Arabic original](https://wipolex-res.wipo.int/edocs/lexdocs/laws/ar/eg/eg065ar.pdf), [English reference translation](https://wipolex-res.wipo.int/edocs/lexdocs/laws/en/eg/eg065en_1.pdf).

Egyptian Customs' pre-clearance manual describes ACI information before shipment and matching the ACID across electronic documents. The importer and forwarder should confirm the route and submission sequence before dispatch, with consistent invoice descriptions, quantities and identifiers. [Customs manual](https://customs.gov.eg/PDF_Docs/CustomsGuidingManuals/18.pdf).

A current SKU-specific commercial duty calculation was not verified here, so no duty percentage or landed margin is quoted. Do not reuse passenger-baggage valuation tables as commercial tariff evidence. Ask the broker for the applicable national tariff line, origin treatment, tax basis and dated calculation alongside the factory quote.

## 7. A purchase brief that a supplier can answer

For each proposed set, require an itemised utensil list, alloy and finish by component, dimensions and mass, handle construction, reference samples, cleaning instructions and evidence supporting food-contact and durability claims. Add the approved Arabic artwork, retail package, master-carton count, barcode and lot-traceability information. Describe any coloured material or coating explicitly instead of equating colour with a manufacturing process.

The supermarket brief should state the shelf footprint, replenishment unit and handling of incomplete packs. The e-commerce brief should state parcel dimensions, scratch protection, inspection photographs, customer-facing contents and the process for incomplete or damaged deliveries. Keep cash collection and order rejection as measured pilot outcomes, not invented industry averages.

This research supports a controlled six-setting assortment test. It does not establish national cutlery market size, a bestselling colour or a ready-to-import SKU. The concentrated sample, unavailable retail-only total, unpurchased full standard and order-specific freight, packaging and tariff questions remain explicit.
