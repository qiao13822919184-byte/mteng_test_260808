A gold or black cutlery sample can be approved for appearance without proving how that finish will perform in the buyer's intended use. If a retailer or hospitality group wants to say a colored finish is durable, the purchasing team needs to define **which finish, on which component, against what exposure and by which evidence**. Do not convert a good-looking sample into an unqualified wear or dishwasher claim.

Yumingxing's [cutlery collection](https://www.mteng.ltd/en/stainless-steel-cutlery/) lists gold and black finish options for discussion, but asks buyers to confirm the selected model, treatment and care instructions. Independent cutlery testing organisation [CATRA's product-testing overview](https://www.catra.org/testing-services/testing-product-evaluation/) lists coating thickness and adhesion evaluations, including for electroplated and PVD finishes, and dishwasher-resistance evaluation among available services. A list of possible tests does not show that a particular Yumingxing cutlery set uses one of those processes or has passed any test.

## Write a claim-to-evidence brief

| Field | Buyer and supplier must identify | Release question |
|---|---|---|
| Product identity | Exact knife, fork or spoon, model and finish revision | Does the evidence cover this piece? |
| Treatment | Actual finishing process, if known and disclosed | Are we naming a process without confirmation? |
| Intended claim | Exact wording for catalogue, box or buyer reply | Is it visual description or a performance promise? |
| Exposure | Intended cleaning, handling and storage conditions | Do the conditions match the buyer's use? |
| Evaluation | Suitable method and measured attribute agreed for that claim | Is appearance approval being mistaken for testing? |
| Test sample | Component, finish revision, date and batch reference | Can the result be linked to the order? |
| Decision | Acceptance criteria, reviewer and unresolved limits | Should the claim be approved, narrowed or withheld? |

Ask the supplier what treatment is actually proposed before requesting a coating-specific test. A gold appearance alone does not prove electroplating or PVD, and a black appearance does not identify its process. If the proposal changes, the old sample or report may no longer cover the ordered version. Where dishwasher resistance matters, define the cleaning conditions and appropriate evaluation separately rather than assuming that color approval covers machine washing.

The [set-wide color approval guide](/en/blog/approve-colored-cutlery-finish-across-a-set/) handles visual consistency across pieces. This brief addresses the separate performance-evidence decision. Keep the exact approved wording and its supporting record with the specification; leave an unsupported durability statement off the listing or pack until the evidence for the actual product is reviewed.
