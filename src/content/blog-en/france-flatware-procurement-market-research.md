For a France flatware launch, buy against a **verified retail unit and an identified seller**, then approve the French information and the evidence supporting each material claim. A low online price without the same contents, finish, delivery terms and documentation is a weak purchasing benchmark.

This study addresses large supermarket buyers and e-commerce sales companies. Sources were accessed on **9 November 2026, Asia/Shanghai**; editorial QA was completed separately. It records **14 available online SKU observations** from IKEA France, Lidl France and Carrefour.fr. IKEA and Lidl are two physical retail-chain references with overlapping own-webshop channels; Carrefour adds a third-party marketplace comparison. These are not five independent retailers. Lidl's observed offers are labelled online-only; Carrefour's four offers are sold by named partners, not Carrefour itself. No shop-floor price, local shelf availability, transaction, consumer survey or laboratory result was independently measured. Prices are displayed consumer offers in **EUR**, before delivery; trade values are current USD. The cover is an **AI-generated editorial illustration**, not a factory photograph, test or specific SKU.

## 1. Demand context: separate retail volume from online transaction value

INSEE's latest available release in this research reports August 2026 retail sales volume, excluding motor vehicles and motorcycles, up **0.3% month on month**. Across June–August, that retail volume was **2.8% above the same period a year earlier**. This seasonally and trading-day adjusted index is not nominal sales revenue. The release was published on **21 October 2026**; its next, September observation was scheduled for **20 November**, so August is not relabelled September. [INSEE retail-volume release](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/9091152).

FEVAD's original annual release puts 2025 online goods and services at **EUR196.4 billion**, up **7%**. Goods alone were **EUR76.1 billion**, up **4%**; services were **EUR120.3 billion**, up **9%**. An online-goods buying plan should not use the combined total as its merchandise market. Neither this national aggregate nor the INSEE index identifies cutlery demand, stainless material share or a particular retailer's sell-through. [FEVAD, 11 February 2026](https://www.fevad.com/bilan-du-e-commerce-en-france-les-francais-ont-depense-pres-de-200-milliards-deuros-sur-internet-en-2025/).

For a supermarket, request household-tableware sales, shelf productivity and replenishment intervals from the actual category team. For an e-commerce company, request order value after delivery, conversion, returns and contribution per parcel. Positive national growth is a reason to test a proposition, not evidence that any observed set will sell profitably.

## 4. Use the observed price ladder to design a pilot, not a market average

Within IKEA alone, current 24-piece offers range from **EUR12.99 to EUR45.00**, with DRAGON at **EUR19.99** and SJUGÄLSHAJ at **EUR24.99** between them. The observed partner-sold 24-piece offers range from **EUR44.79 to EUR119.00**. These are small, selected retail observations containing different designs, brands, packing and services; they are not nationwide price bands or wholesale target quotes.

For a supermarket pilot, a conventional **24-piece, six-person, four-type** specification is supported by several explicit contents lists. Use one entry proposition and one demonstrably differentiated alternative, rather than importing several visually similar sets with uncertain material claims. A **30-piece set with dessert forks** is a separate test proposition. The choice is an inference from the assortment, not a consumer-preference survey.

For online selling, compare the ordinary set, the coffee-spoon replenishment pack and larger configurations on **delivered contribution**, not price per piece alone. Include acquisition cost, import clearance, fulfilment, marketplace fees, delivery subsidy and returns using your own actual quotations. None is supplied by the product pages. The 56-piece and 60-piece sets have different place-setting structures and cannot be ranked just by count.

## 5. French information and food-contact evidence are different checks

Article 2 of France's language law requires French for product designation, offers, presentation, instructions, guarantee terms and invoices. Article 4 requires the French presentation to be as legible or intelligible as accompanying translations. Approve the **pack, insert and online text together**: exact contents, materials by component, care instructions and the relevant economic operator. A French headline with untranslated care information is not a complete localisation review. [Law94-665, Articles2 and4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000349929/).

The EU food-contact framework requires articles, under normal or foreseeable use, not to transfer constituents at harmful levels or cause unacceptable changes to food. Traceability and applicable labelling remain relevant. GMP addresses controlled manufacture and documentation; it is not proof that an individual retail SKU has passed testing. [Regulation1935/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02004R1935-20210327); [Regulation2023/2006](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02006R2023-20250316).

For uncoated stainless steel, the French regulator DGCCRF reproduces the composition criteria of the **13 January 1976 order**, including a minimum chromium content of **13.0%**. Its guidance asks the steel producer to supply composition analysis and written conformity to the finished-product manufacturer. The order's composition approach does **not** mean that all migration or general safety duties disappear. Coated, thermochemically treated or mixed-material articles require the relevant additional assessment. A rose-coloured finish or a plastic handle should not be waved through using an uncoated-steel sheet alone. [DGCCRF stainless-steel guidance](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgccrf/Acier-inoxydable); [Official1976 order index](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000000637961).

The accessible regulator text supports that composition summary; the original historical journal scan was not retrievable in this run. It is not represented here as a downloaded full legal text. The exact product's applicability, material evidence and test conditions must be agreed with the responsible operator and competent laboratory before ordering.

## 6. Do not turn a buyer's document request into a universal legal claim

Current Article6 of Decree2007-766 calls for a written conformity declaration at commercial stages other than final-consumer supply, but expressly excepts materials whose characteristics make their food-contact purpose obvious. Therefore, do **not** state that every metal cutlery set necessarily needs the same statutory declaration at every sale. The raw-steel documentation discussed by DGCCRF and a retailer's own contractual evidence requirement are separate issues. [Current Article6](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000020062888).

As a buying condition, request a component-level material schedule, traceable composition evidence, finish specification, intended-use conditions and test reports matched to the supplied model. That is a proposed purchasing control, not a claim that a competitor's offer already meets it. For remote sales, assess the GPSR's applicable offer-information requirements, including manufacturer and, where required, EU responsible-person details, product identification and relevant warnings; account for its interaction with sector-specific food-contact rules. [GPSR2023/988, Articles2 and19](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj/eng).

For import planning, identify the customs operator and EORI, then use French customs' RITA route to confirm the declaration code, origin and date-specific measures. HS6 research headings do not establish the finished set's full tariff classification, duty rate or preferential origin. No universal duty or landed margin is asserted here. [French customs EORI](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/fiche/le-numero-eori-economic-operator-registration-and-identification); [French customs RITA](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/service-en-ligne/tarif-douanier-communautaire-et-national-rita).

Before printing packaging, determine the applicable household-packaging producer-responsibility and sorting-information route and who is responsible. ArticleL541-9-3 connects sorting signage and instructions to products within its EPR scope, with stated exceptions; it is not a generic logo requirement for every steel utensil. Agree the applicable artwork and components, rather than copying a competitor's marks. [Environmental Code ArticleL541-9-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000041556010).

## 7. Separate the supermarket and e-commerce buying decisions

| Decision | Large supermarket buyer | E-commerce sales company |
|---|---|---|
| Initial assortment | Test a clearly specified24-piece core and one meaningful upgrade; validate against actual shelf productivity | Test configurations separately; disclose setting count and every utensil type |
| Packaging | Request final retail-pack dimensions, barcode visibility, French insert and case-pack quantity | Request actual shipment dimensions, gross weight and protective packing; do not substitute product weight |
| Evidence acceptance | Match component grades and finish to the approved reference sample and supplied batch | Make title, specifications, images, seller and responsibility information consistent |
| Replenishment | Use sell-through, stockouts and verified supplier lead time; no invented national order volume | Use delivered contribution, returns and fulfilment performance; recheck partner offers and prices |

These are proposed pilot controls derived from the evidence, not guarantees of demand or supplier capability. A retailer's accepted shelf pack may need additional parcel protection. Conversely, an oversized presentation box can consume shelf and shipping space without adding enough customer value; obtain measured dimensions before deciding.

## 8. Turn the research into a comparable quotation

Send shortlisted suppliers a common brief: exact utensil bill of materials and quantities; length, weight and finish by item; component alloy evidence; intended food-contact use and care wording; French artwork; retail-pack and export-carton measurements; case quantity; barcode allocation; MOQ; sample and production lead times; quotation validity; and delivery term. Keep **verified facts, buyer specifications and unanswered questions** in separate fields.

The unresolved commercial inputs are actual French category sales, local store stock, postal-code checkout, independently verified alloy and testing, transport packing, tariff treatment and each operator's packaging obligations. These gaps prevent a defensible national market-size estimate or firm order forecast, but they do not prevent a disciplined sample comparison. Start with documented configurations, approve the evidence and French information, then scale only against channel results.
