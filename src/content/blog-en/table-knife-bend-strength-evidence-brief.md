“The knife feels solid” is useful sample feedback, but it is not a measured bend-strength result. For a table-knife order where mechanical strength is a formal purchase requirement, the buyer should decide **which knife configuration and performance question the evidence must address** before asking for a report. A test of another blade or an informal hand-flex check cannot silently become evidence for the proposed order.

Independent cutlery testing organisation [CATRA describes table cutlery evaluation](https://www.catra.org/testing-services/testing-product-evaluation/) and provides a [knife and flatware bend-strength tester overview](https://www.catra.org/testing-equipment/knife-edges/cutlery-bend-tester/). The latter explains a controlled load and measurement of permanent deformation in its test arrangement. These pages show that bend strength can be evaluated; they do not state that a Yumingxing knife has been tested or passed. Yumingxing's [quality guidance](https://www.mteng.ltd/en/quality-compliance/) asks buyers to agree product-specific inspection requirements and criteria in writing.

## Define the request before seeking a result

| Evidence field | Buyer should specify or ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Knife identity | Model, blade/handle construction, material, finish and revision | A different knife is not the ordered sample |
| Purpose | Design comparison, routine quality check or contractual acceptance | The decision changes the evidence needed |
| Method | Applicable method and qualified evaluator agreed for this purpose | A hand-flex impression is not a controlled test |
| Test conditions | Fixture, load, direction and sample conditioning as defined by the method | Results depend on the arrangement |
| Samples | Count, selection, lot link and identification | One result should not be generalized to every shipment |
| Criterion | Acceptance limit and who sets it before testing | Avoid judging after seeing a number |
| Report | Method, sample ID, measured result, date, issuer and deviations | Enables a reviewer to check scope and traceability |

This is a request template, not a recommended load, pass limit or mandated standard. Ask a qualified test provider or the buyer's technical team to select the appropriate method and criterion for the actual product and destination. A report should not be presented as evidence of corrosion resistance, dishwasher suitability or food-contact compliance simply because it concerns the same knife.

An ordinary dinner-knife sample trial addresses use with the intended meal; this brief addresses formal strength evidence. If the knife construction, material or revision changes, ask whether the old report still applies and re-evaluate the affected requirement before releasing a strength claim.
