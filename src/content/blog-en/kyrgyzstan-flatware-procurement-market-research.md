Kyrgyzstan offers a useful case for testing a distributor-led, tightly specified flatware range. Public offers show both individually sold utensils and household sets. They also show why grouping purchases across channels should start with matching the physical product and its paperwork: a marketplace listing can repeat the same chain's stock, while a “36-piece set” can contain no dinner knives.

For large supermarkets, the immediate question is whether a compact assortment can be replenished reliably in the required cities. For e-commerce sales companies, it is whether complete contents, an actual packed weight and an executable fulfilment quote support the listed price. Pooling orders may reduce some fixed costs, but this research has no supplier quotation demonstrating an actual saving.

Product observations were recorded on **2–3 November 2026, Asia/Shanghai**; the label-language source was checked on **5 November 2026**, and the national conformity documents on **6 November 2026**. Prices are **Kyrgyzstani som (KGS)** unless stated; trade values are current USD. This is public-source desk research without store visits, interviews, checkout purchases or product tests. The cover is an AI editorial illustration of generic stainless-steel cutlery and cartons, not a particular supplier, retailer SKU or actual order.

## 1. National retail and e-commerce: keep the denominators separate

The National Statistical Committee's open table measures **retail turnover including catering**, in **million KGS**. Its enterprise/market split is not a modern-chain/e-commerce split.

| Year | Total | Trading enterprises | Markets |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023 | 771,972.3 | 499,324.7 | 272,647.6 |
| 2024 | 927,882.5 | 645,243.0 | 282,639.5 |
| 2025 | 1,228,511.0 | 808,955.7 | 419,555.3 |

The series establishes the scale of broad trading activity, not household flatware spending. These current-price values do not isolate volume growth or stainless-steel sales. They cannot support multiplying the national total by an assumed cutlery share. [National retail table](https://stat.gov.kg/ru/opendata/category/2417/).

The official e-commerce methodology defines transactions around digital ordering; payment and delivery may occur online or offline, and domestic and cross-border transactions are distinguished. It identifies annual reporting instruments, including the e-commerce report. A methodology document is not an observed annual market total. [National e-commerce methodology, pages4–7](https://stat.gov.kg/ru/statistics/download/methodology/211/).

On 30 October 2025, the Kyrgyz Cabinet reported an estimate of USD 525 million for domestic e-commerce in 2025. It also reported about 1 million online purchase transactions worth approximately KGS 1.7 billion in the first half of 2025. The annual estimate was issued before year-end. The first-half figures describe a different period and measure. Use these figures to assess the digital channel as a whole. A flatware order still needs category-specific sales data from the retailer. [Cabinet statement](https://www.gov.kg/ru/post/s/25755-ministrler-kabinetinin-toragasy-adylbek-kasymaliev-e-commerce-expo-central-asia-2025-elektronduk-kommerciya-boyunca-el-aralyk-forumunun-isine-katysty).

## 2. Three-year imports are a screening signal, not market size

UN Comtrade observations use Kyrgyzstan as reporter 417, imports, partner 0/world and **H6/HS2022**. CIF values include the import valuation basis; they are not retail receipts.

| Year | HS 8215, USD | HS 821191, USD | HS 8215 net weight, kg |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023 | 259,093 | 39,697 | 146,968 |
| 2024 | 195,779 | 14,760 | 56,380.595* |
| 2025 | 310,110 | 17,228 | 59,773.142* |

*Comtrade marks these world net weights as estimated. HS 821191 world weights are also estimated in 2023 and 2024; do not calculate an authoritative landed price per kilogram from those fields. Raw returned observations and estimation flags are preserved. [2023 query](https://comtradeapi.un.org/public/v1/preview/C/A/HS?period=2023&reporterCode=417&cmdCode=8215,821191&flowCode=M&partnerCode=0,156,643,398), [2024 query](https://comtradeapi.un.org/public/v1/preview/C/A/HS?period=2024&reporterCode=417&cmdCode=8215,821191&flowCode=M&partnerCode=0,156,643,398), [2025 query](https://comtradeapi.un.org/public/v1/preview/C/A/HS?period=2025&reporterCode=417&cmdCode=8215,821191&flowCode=M&partnerCode=0,156,643,398).

HS 8215 includes spoons, forks and other kitchen/table articles in multiple materials. HS 821191 concerns fixed-blade table knives. The two lines are shown separately and do not define a stainless-steel retail market. Changes may reflect assortment, recording, valuation and routing, not simply household demand.

For HS 8215, China-coded imports were USD 160,535 in 2023, USD 70,789 in 2024 and USD 60,822 in 2025. The 2025 observations for Kazakhstan and Russia were USD 11,049 and USD 32,157 respectively, against the world total of USD 310,110. These selected partners are not an exhaustive supplier ranking. A regional trade partner does not establish where a particular utensil was manufactured. Nor does the decline in the China-coded series prove consumers prefer another origin.

The purchasing implication is a **testable small-range hypothesis**, rather than a national unit-sales forecast. Ask a distributor for actual customer orders, inventory age, documented inbound routes and replenishment economics before allocating a container to the country.

## 4. Two physical channels and two marketplaces do not mean independent stock

Planeta's current contact page lists three retail locations in Bishkek and one in Osh, separately from its service centre. The observed RD-245 and RD-1782 pages showed availability in Bishkek but not Osh; RD-1157 and RD-244 showed both cities. This supports checking allocation by city, without assuming the website's stock labels measure branch-level quantities. [Planeta official locations](https://planeta.kg/contacts).

Globus provides a second physical-chain context and an online grocery offer. Its single-spoon page did not have a delivery address selected. The main chain's background page could not be retrieved directly, so this study does not repeat a current store-count claim. Neither chain sample is evidence of shelf sell-through.

BAKAI Store lists RD-245, RD-1157 and RD-244 at the same observed prices as Planeta, with **Planeta Elektroniki as seller**. These are paired channel observations of the same models, not three additional competing manufacturers. Stock should not be added across the two interfaces without confirmation that fulfilment pools are separate. [BAKAI RD-245](https://bakai.store/dom-i-dacha/nabor-stolovyh-priborov-24-predmeta-rondell-rd-1056755), [RD-1157](https://bakai.store/dom-i-dacha/nabor-stolovyh-priborov-rondell-rd-1157-aurora-1057093), [RD-244](https://bakai.store/dom-i-dacha/nabor-stolovyh-priborov-rondell-rd-244-1057456).

The BAKAI information panel labels some package dimensions and weights as AI estimates. Those values are excluded from freight calculations. Planeta's 0 kg field is also not a measured physical weight. A real packed sample is needed.

Bazar Market describes a marketplace connecting Dordoi sellers, with delivery/pickup services. Four 36-piece, KGS 2,725 pages from seller Posuda have matching descriptions; this research consolidates them into one offer family. Two lower-priced six-person listings also need comparison before they can be treated as different physical products. A quantity selector showing 1/10 or 1/5 does not prove a wholesale MOQ or negotiated discount. [Platform description](https://docs.bazarmarket.kg/).

Additional DNS Tima 24-piece and Sulpak RD-246 24-piece pages were unavailable at observation, without a usable current price. They remain specification references only. The 799-som recommendation elsewhere on the DNS page belonged to another knife, not the Tima set. [DNS reference](https://dns-shop.kg/ru/product/204b52abf368ed20/nabor-stolovyh-priborov-tima-fuzn-05451ss-24-st/), [Sulpak reference](https://www.sulpak.kg/g/nabor_stoloviyh_priborov_24_predmeta__rondell_rd_246).

## 5. Import, conformity and product information

The EEC chapter 82 tariff PDF dated 8 March 2026 contains the following lines. These are the rates printed in that schedule, not a product-specific customs decision or an all-in tax quote.

| Candidate code | Description, abbreviated | Schedule duty |
|---|---|---:|
| 8215201000 | Other sets of corrosion-resistant steel | 12% |
| 8215991000 | Other corrosion-resistant-steel articles | 15% |
| 8211910001 | Fixed-blade table knives with stainless handle and blade | 15% |

Chapter 82 note 3 also addresses classification of qualifying mixed sets containing knives. Exact contents, surface treatment, origin, preferences and the rules effective at entry must be checked before choosing a code. A distributor's location in an EAEU country does not by itself settle origin, customs status or tax treatment. [EEC original schedule, note3 and pages8–10](https://eec.eaeunion.org/comission/department/catr/ett/ru.2022/ru.82_2022_08.03.2026.pdf).

The Justice Ministry's current register lists corrosion-resistant-steel table articles, kitchen sets and cutlery in **row 18 of Annex 2 to Cabinet Resolution 267**. The entry excludes products for children under three and lists six 8215 tariff lines, including 8215201000 and 8215991000. It provides for **certification or declaration**, with references to GOST R 51687-2000 clauses 5.15, 5.17, 5.24, 5.25 and section 8. The list's latest displayed edition is 22 May 2024. Its closing note calls for later editions of revised standards. This entry gives buyers a specific starting point; it does not automatically cover a separately classified 8211 knife. [National product list, row 18](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/230010630/edition/12782/ru).

The operative procedure, Annex 1 to Resolution 267, includes the 28 August 2025 amendment. Sections 3.3–3.5 distinguish accredited certification bodies, declaration-registration bodies and laboratories, whose accredited scope must match the work required by the selected scheme. Section 3.10 directs declaration registration to Resolution 20. The latter's current text includes the **29 June 2026** amendment; its paragraph 2 allows declaration for products on the 267 list where that form is specified. [Current conformity procedure](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/159202/edition/37355/ru) · [Resolution 20, current edition](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/12908/edition/56439/ru).

The national declaration procedure requires supporting evidence and registration. Its paragraph 7 addresses business registration, relevant test/measurement evidence and the agreement assigning responsibilities when acting for a foreign manufacturer; paragraph 8 makes the declaration effective upon registration. A supplier's unsigned template or material description is not a registered declaration. Annex 3 to Resolution 267, sections 3.3–3.4, requires a copy of the registered declaration for the declaration-based customs route, alongside completion of the other customs procedures. [National declaration procedure](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/12909/edition/1195604/ru) · [Import procedure](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/159203/edition/1189851/ru).

**Food contact and standard versions need their own procurement line.** The original 2000 standard's clause 5.25 addresses migration into food simulants, while clause 7.16 addresses analysis and extraction conditions. Corrosion resistance and a seller's “18/10” statement cannot substitute for that evidence. An original-edition scan is available through a public standards mirror; its age is material here. [GOST R 51687-2000, clauses 5.25 and 7.16, original scan](https://files.stroyinf.ru/Data2/1/4294814/4294814225.pdf).

Rosstandart records GOST R 51687-2021 as the replacement, effective in Russia from 1 March 2022. The Kyrgyz list still prints the old designation alongside its later-edition note. Before commissioning tests, obtain a written, model-specific mapping from the Kyrgyz conformity body: applicable edition, current clauses, food-contact materials, simulants, exposure conditions and acceptance limits. Do not carry old clause numbers or numeric limits into a new report by assumption, or treat a Russian status record as approval of a Kyrgyz consignment. [Rosstandart replacement record](https://protect.gost.ru/gost/details/df7bd607-82bc-442d-8dd6-5109a1b63f5f).

The EEC's original section 16 on food-contact materials includes some stainless-steel table/kitchen articles under 7323 93 in its scope table; 8215 is not listed in that table. Its steel migration table therefore cannot, by itself, establish the correct test basis for every 8215 set or 8211 knife. Identify the actual product and each food-contact material before applying it. The specific standard-version mapping and shipment documents remain **pre-order confirmation items**, not claims that the sampled products have passed testing. [EEC food-contact text, scope and materials tables](https://eec.eaeunion.org/upload/files/depsanmer/P2_299/%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%B4%D0%B5%D0%BB%2016%20%D0%95%D0%A1%D0%A2.pdf).

The National Language Commission publishes Constitutional Law No. 140 of 17 July 2023 with the amendments of 9 August 2025, No. 194. Article 14 requires product information—including label text, markings, product lists and instructions—in the state and official languages, Kyrgyz and Russian. It assigns the cost of translating foreign-language product information into Kyrgyz to the importing firm. Protected trademarks, service marks, appellations of origin and geographical indications retain their legally protected form. These are the law’s provisions, separate from the commentary printed below them. [Commission legal text, Article 14, page 16](https://mamtil.gov.kg/api/files/3urz9yu4041o98u/4kly37pm2re53n6/dEt6h0lNjv_76mzmloXSp..pdf).

For the procurement brief, ask for one reconciled Kyrgyz/Russian contents and care sheet and identify who pays for translation before approving the carton artwork. Keeping a protected brand name in its protected form does not replace translating the accompanying product description. This verifies the language requirement; it does not approve a particular label, establish every mandatory label field or replace the model-specific conformity and food-contact documents above.

## 6. A supermarket procurement brief

**Inference from the observed offers:** start evaluation with a single-unit replenishment option and one clearly specified household set; add a gift presentation only if the buyer's own sales and shelf economics support it. This is a proposed trial structure, not a measured local preference.

For the set, require a contents sheet with one line for each utensil, actual length and weight, steel grade claim supported by appropriate documentation, finish, care instructions and replacement policy. Have the buyer count a sealed sample. The distinction between a knife-containing household set and a three-type36-piece assortment must remain visible on the shelf label.

Request separate allocations and delivery quotes for Bishkek and Osh. Ask for actual stock, carton multiples, partial-carton rules, dispatch lead time and the point at which delivery is complete. Public “many/few” labels and the prompt to contact Planeta for more than 3 units are neither a guaranteed wholesale capacity nor an agreed minimum order.

Evaluate a common inner retail pack with a channel-specific outer label or sleeve only after label compliance is cleared. Each saleable configuration needs an unambiguous identifier. A carton must list inner units and utensil counts so that pooled purchasing does not create picking mistakes. The buyer should set the trial quantity from its own shelf space, observed sell-through and replenishment time; this research supplies none of those internal numbers.

## 7. An e-commerce procurement brief

Use the exact same counted contents in the title, photographs, attributes and packing slip. Show whether knives are included and distinguish utensils from place settings. Do not copy another seller's generic “6-person” description into an unverified listing.

For each candidate, obtain a measured packed weight and dimensions, then price a normal parcel and a return. Request commission, payment, fulfilment, cancellation and return terms from the selected channel. BAKAI's displayed city/date options are an observation, not a national service-level commitment. No margin should be approved using an AI dimension estimate.

Keep one shared inventory ledger if the same distributor supplies its own site and a marketplace. Reserve stock when orders are accepted and reconcile cancellations before promising a dispatch date. This is a proposed operational control prompted by the observed Planeta/BAKAI overlap, not a description of their internal systems.

Choose listing tests that answer a procurement question: explicit knife inclusion versus a three-type set; counted contents versus ambiguous place-setting language; a measured parcel-ready pack versus a gift box with unknown freight dimensions. Record actual conversion, returns, damaged units and contribution per delivered order. Do not infer those outcomes from public listing prices.

## 8. When pooled orders could help—and when they could add cost

Ask suppliers for written quotations against the **same specification**, comparing a stand-alone order with a pooled order. Separate product price, packaging setup, inspection/testing, inland and international freight, customs handling, taxes, local delivery, storage and financing. Allocation of fixed charges across more accepted saleable units may lower unit cost; more sorting, relabelling, holding stock or extra delivery legs may offset it.

A workable evaluation is:

**Landed cost per accepted saleable unit = total attributable purchasing and delivery costs / accepted saleable units.**

Do not substitute the number of spoons for the number of retail sets, use Comtrade unit values as supplier quotes, or allocate recoverable tax without the buyer's accounting treatment. Resolve mixed-SKU carton counts, legal importer responsibility, common documentation, rejection allocation and replenishment priority in writing before combining purchases.

For the first quotation round, specify the utensil count, care instructions, saleable pack and receiving city. Compare the distributor’s separate and pooled quotes on those same terms. Approve label artwork and release documents only after the importer has reconciled the applicable standard edition, registered conformity evidence and the actual saleable pack.
