*Research checked 16 November 2026. Public product observations: 13 and 16 November; prices in Brazilian reais (BRL), before any destination-specific delivery charge. This is desk research for supermarket procurement and e-commerce sales teams, not a consumer survey or a forecast of flatware sales.*

*Cover: AI-generated editorial illustration of stainless steel knives, forks and spoons with generic packaging. It does not depict a factory, tested product, customer order or specific SKU.*

Brazil merits a tightly costed trial, but the evidence does not justify a national rollout built around “more pieces for less money.” Imports in relevant customs headings increased, while recent general retail growth was modest. Local listings place all-steel sets beside cheaper polypropylene-handled alternatives, and the same branded set can carry materially different payment prices. The first buying decision is therefore a **defined bill of materials, payment basis and delivered contribution per set**, followed by a measured store or postcode pilot.

## 1. Import growth is a sourcing signal, not market size

The following annual [2023](https://comtradeapi.un.org/public/v1/preview/C/A/HS?period=2023&reporterCode=76&cmdCode=8215,821191&flowCode=M&partnerCode=0,156), [2024](https://comtradeapi.un.org/public/v1/preview/C/A/HS?period=2024&reporterCode=76&cmdCode=8215,821191&flowCode=M&partnerCode=0,156) and [2025](https://comtradeapi.un.org/public/v1/preview/C/A/HS?period=2025&reporterCode=76&cmdCode=8215,821191&flowCode=M&partnerCode=0,156) UN Comtrade observations use Brazil as reporter, imports, world and China as partners, and HS2022/H6 throughout. Values are CIF US dollars, shown in millions; shares are calculated from unrounded values.

| Year | HS8215 world, USD million | China value / share | HS821191 world, USD million | China value / share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023 | 27.013 | 22.331 / 82.7% | 3.346 | 2.732 / 81.7% |
| 2024 | 36.979 | 33.252 / 89.9% | 5.602 | 4.968 / 88.7% |
| 2025 | 48.672 | 43.436 / 89.2% | 6.460 | 5.920 / 91.6% |

HS8215 world imports rose **80.2%** between 2023 and 2025; China's share increased from **82.7% to 89.2%**. Fixed-blade table-knife imports under HS821191 rose **93.1%** over those years. These are changes in import value, not unit demand, stainless-steel market size or retail growth. Exchange rates, freight, mix and inventories can affect the relationship between imports and final sell-through.

HS8215 includes several materials, plated articles and related utensils. Table knives are not fully contained in it; mixed assortments may require another classification. Do not add the two series and label the result “Brazil's stainless flatware market.” Estimated net weights occur in the source records, and quantities are not a reliable comparable count of complete sets. We therefore calculate neither imported sets nor an average price per piece.

Brazil's [official Comex Stat data documentation](https://www.gov.br/mdic/pt-br/assuntos/comercio-exterior/estatisticas/base-de-dados-bruta) distinguishes FOB merchandise value from freight and insurance. Its national extracts were not independently reconciled cell by cell with these twelve Comtrade records. This is a documented limitation: a FOB figure from Comex Stat must not be silently substituted into the CIF series. The [current official NCM nomenclature](https://portalunico.siscomex.gov.br/classif/api/publico/nomenclatura/download/json) also distinguishes other sets, 8215.20.00, from other stainless articles, 8215.99.10, and fixed-blade table knives, 8211.91.00. Final classification depends on the actual assortment.

## 2. Retail and e-commerce point to disciplined testing

IBGE's [September 2026 retail report](https://ftp.ibge.gov.br/Comercio_e_Servicos/Pesquisa_Mensal_de_Comercio/Fasciculo_Indicadores_IBGE/pmc_202609caderno.pdf), released on 13 November, records seasonally adjusted retail volume growth of **0.2%** from August and **1.7%** against September 2025. The hypermarket, supermarket, food, beverage and tobacco grouping fell **0.5%** month on month. These are broad business-activity measures, not flatware-category results. They support monitoring a test closely; they do not establish that supermarket cutlery sales are falling.

For online selling, [MDIC's published 2024 results](https://www.gov.br/mdic/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2025/junho/vendas-de-pequenas-empresas-pela-internet-crescem-1-200-desde-a-pandemia-mostra-painel-do-mdic) put domestic e-commerce at **BRL225 billion**, up **14.6%**, with **77.2%** of sales attributed to sellers in the Southeast but **55.9%** to buyers there. Use the buyer location when designing service coverage; seller concentration is not a map of final demand.

The [MDIC methodology](https://www.gov.br/mdic/pt-br/assuntos/sdic/observatorio/perguntas-frequentes) covers relevant NF-e electronic invoices, internet transactions, CNPJ business issuers and CPF individual recipients. It excludes direct cross-border consumer imports, exports, B2B transactions and digital services. Consequently, BRL225 billion is neither all marketplace GMV nor a measure of flatware sales. It is explicitly a 2024 observation; newer downloadable files are available, but were not re-aggregated for this study.

For a buyer, the practical implication is to keep three records separate: importer purchase value, domestic consumer invoice value and actual units sold by the proposed SKU. None can substitute for the others when deciding a replenishment order.

Package dimensions are manufacturer-listed retail-pack height × width × length, not export-carton dimensions. “Undisclosed” means no usable field was obtained. Steel grades are undisclosed in these observations; “stainless” is a seller or manufacturer claim, not an independent laboratory result. Plastic-handled rows are deliberately retained as budget comparators and must not be presented as all-steel sets.

Three comparisons matter more than a single average price:

- **Same product, different purchase conditions.** Malibu 60 was BRL219.90 at Milium and BRL219.60 at Zaffari on 13 November, versus BRL150.16 by Pix or BRL168.72 by credit/boleto in the direct shop. The Pix quote is 31.7% below Milium's displayed quote, before freight. That does not measure retailer margin: promotion funding, payment timing, tax treatment and service differ.
- **Piece count does not determine tier.** The direct-shop Búzios 60 was BRL154.90 by Pix, while Zurique 24 was BRL429.40. A buyer must compare weight, finish, knife design and composition before describing a 60-piece offer as better value.
- **A low headline price may describe a different construction.** Ipanema 20 and Leme 20 have polypropylene handles, unlike the all-steel rows. A private-label specification must state blade and handle materials separately. For New Azul 24, the category description identifies six each of knives, forks, spoons and teaspoons; the approved supplier bill of materials still takes precedence.

Zaffari labels its Malibu description as AI-generated. Its detailed composition was not accepted as an approved manufacturer's bill of materials. This is a concrete reason to verify every utensil type and count rather than copying a retailer paragraph into a purchase order. The separately checked Leme 12 listing was out of stock and is excluded from the fourteen usable offers.

## 4. A supermarket assortment should earn its space

**Recommended trial hypothesis:** begin with one all-steel everyday set in the 16–24-piece range and one clearly differentiated larger or premium set. This is an editorial proposal based on the sample, not evidence that those sizes lead Brazilian sales. The sample supports several tiers, so a chain need not duplicate every family or colour at launch.

At a range meeting, compare candidate sets with the visible Laguna 16, Búzios 24 and Maresias 24 references. Ask whether the proposed landed cost leaves room for the chain's actual shelf price, promotional calendar and service costs. A “premium” claim needs a visible and documented reason—such as a verified finish, component weight or knife construction—not an inferred steel grade.

For large sets, print the exact component counts on the Portuguese pack. “60 pieces” is not automatically twelve identical five-piece settings: different families can use different mixes, and the Malibu composition remains a verification item in this sample. Do not photograph a larger collection than the retail pack contains.

Require a physical packed sample before approving shelf depth and case quantity. The observed Maresias 24 box has a 47cm longest side; the Búzios 60 box is 35cm long. More pieces do not necessarily require the longer box. Confirm hanging versus shelf presentation, barcode placement, protection of knife tips, scuff protection, case gross weight and the number of retail packs per case. These are proposed buyer acceptance criteria, not claimed legal carton standards.

A practical trial record should capture store, shelf facings, stock received, weekly units sold, stockouts, promotional days and damage returns. Replenish only after distinguishing low demand from missing stock or poor display. No sell-through threshold or opening quantity can be supported from the public listings alone.

## 5. Online economics depend on payment and postcode

Keep Pix and instalment offers in separate comparison columns. Do not subtract advertised cashback from the purchase price unless the actual redemption conditions and customer eligibility have been verified. The sample contains strong promotions; regular-price and campaign performance should be measured separately.

[Milium's delivery terms](https://www.milium.com.br/entrega-e-retiradas), checked during its November campaign, offer free freight from **BRL299.90** in specified states and categories. Coverage includes SC, PR, RS, MG and SP, with category exclusions outside SC; postcode and basket confirmation remain necessary. It is not a Brazilian universal free-shipping threshold. A single BRL219.90 Malibu set does not reach that advertised basket minimum.

Use the manufacturer's retail-pack dimensions as a starting point, then measure the protective shipping carton. For example, Malibu's listed pack is **26.7×5.2×24.8cm, 1.365kg**, whereas Maresias is **3.5×22.5×47cm, 0.930kg**. A lighter item may still require a more awkward parcel. No carrier dimensional-weight divisor or nationwide freight charge has been assumed here.

For an online pilot, choose one payment-price basis, two or more actual destination zones and one agreed packaging design. Record conversion, delivered contribution, late delivery, missing pieces, scratches and returns. Publish component counts, dimensions and material distinctions in text as well as photographs. Keep proof of the packed contents so a return for “wrong number of spoons” can be investigated against the ordered specification.

## 6. Import and product claims need a dated dossier

The [Receita Federal Classif service](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/assuntos/aduana-e-comercio-exterior/classificacao-fiscal-de-mercadorias/classif) provides classification, tax and administrative-treatment information. The [official import guidance](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/assuntos/aduana-e-comercio-exterior/manuais/subportais-aduana-e-comercio-exterior/intervenientes/como-importar-ou-exportar) distinguishes federal charges from state ICMS. Before a purchase order, have the Brazilian importer confirm the NCM, origin, declaration date, applicable rates and bases, tax credits, licensing and any product-specific measures. This study does not supply a supposedly universal import-tax percentage.

Do not transplant an old trade-remedy rate into today's quotation. Check the [current MDIC measures register](https://www.gov.br/mdic/pt-br/assuntos/comercio-exterior/defesa-comercial-e-interesse-publico/medidas-em-vigor/medidas-em-vigor) and the product's legal scope; an absence of an obvious search match is not clearance. Likewise, [Inmetro's import-consent guidance](https://www.gov.br/inmetro/pt-br/assuntos/regulamentacao/anuencia-para-importacao/como-solicitar) requires checking the product and administrative treatment. It does not establish that every ordinary flatware set requires the same certification.

For metal food-contact components, [ANVISA RDC854/2024](https://anvisalegis.datalegis.net/action/ActionDatalegis.php?acao=abrirTextoAto&cod_menu=9431&cod_modulo=310&link=S&numeroAto=00000854&orgao=RDC%2FDC%2FANVISA%2FMS&seqAto=000&tipo=RDC&valorAno=2024) took effect on 2 May 2024 and revoked RDC20/2007 and RDC498/2021. It addresses permitted materials and food-contact performance, including migration and unacceptable effects on food. The positive list includes several stainless alloys; the rule is not “only 304 is allowed.” Obtain component-level material specifications and applicable test evidence. A grade name alone proves neither final-product conformity nor coating suitability. Plastic components and intended uses require their own applicable assessment.

[Consumer Code Article31](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/lei/1990/lei-8078-11-setembro-1990-365086-normaatualizada-pl.html) requires clear Portuguese product information, including composition, quantity, price, origin and relevant risks. Article49 provides a seven-day withdrawal period for qualifying purchases outside the business premises. [Decree7962/2013](https://www.mppi.mp.br/internet/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/decreto%20n%207962%20-%20regulamenta%20o%20comercio%20eletronico.pdf), Article2, adds online information such as supplier identification, contact address, extra charges and delivery/payment conditions. A foreign-language box or an attractive photograph does not replace the information the Brazilian seller must provide.

Treat these as a dossier checklist, not a claim that any observed SKU or Yumingxing product has been certified. The importer should approve the actual label, listing, materials and supporting documents before shipment.

## 7. Work backwards from a viable delivered offer

Use one BRL worksheet per SKU and selling channel:

**Maximum economically acceptable landed product cost = net sales receipts − channel and fulfilment costs − expected return/defect provision − required contribution.**

“Net” must be defined consistently with the buyer's tax and credit treatment, so taxes are not deducted twice. Then compare the result with a separately costed import quotation: merchandise, international freight/insurance, applicable non-recoverable charges, clearance, domestic transport, packaging and financing. A cost line without a verified quotation remains blank rather than being filled with an invented industry percentage.

For illustration only, BRL100 of net receipts less BRL20 of channel/fulfilment costs, BRL5 of provisions and BRL25 of required contribution leaves **BRL50** for landed product cost. These are hypothetical inputs, not Brazilian market margins, taxes or carrier rates. The calculation is useful because it exposes what must be quoted before a cheap ex-factory price becomes an order.

The first enquiry should therefore include the target channel and state, exact utensil bill of materials, required Portuguese artwork, retail and export pack specifications, test-document requirements, expected delivery terms and a dated cost calculation. Ask [Yumingxing's team](/contact/) for a specification-based quotation and the documents available for the proposed item. MOQ, tooling, lead time, alloy and test evidence must be confirmed for that item; they are not established by this market study.
