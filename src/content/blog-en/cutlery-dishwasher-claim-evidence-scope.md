“Dishwasher safe” sounds like a property of the whole set. A bulk buyer still needs to ask whether the claim covers the **actual knife, fork, spoon, finish and handle construction** being ordered under the intended wash conditions. A sample that fits a wash basket, as discussed in our [loading check](/en/blog/cutlery-dishwashing-basket-loading-sample-check/), does not demonstrate resistance to repeated washing.

CATRA lists [dishwasher resistance among its cutlery testing services](https://www.catra.org/testing-services/testing-product-evaluation/) and describes a [programmable dishwasher-resistance test machine](https://www.catra.org/testing-equipment/cookware-saucepans/dishwasher-resistance-tester/). These pages show that a defined evaluation can be requested; they do **not** prove that any Yumingxing model has been tested or passed. The buyer should agree the relevant method and acceptance criteria with a qualified lab and the destination market reviewer.

## Write the test request before printing the claim

| Question | Record in the request | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which pieces? | Exact knife, fork and spoon models, dimensions, material and handle construction | A result for one component is not automatically a result for the whole set |
| Which finish? | Polished, colored or coated finish, logo and approved sample revision | A changed surface may need a new evidence decision |
| Which wash? | Machine type, program, water and detergent assumptions, cycle count and drying conditions | “Dishwasher” alone does not define exposure |
| What is observed? | Corrosion, loss of gloss, marking, coating or handle change and agreed acceptance limits | Observations need a decision rule |
| Which report? | Lab or responsible tester, sample IDs, dates, method, results and exclusions | The claim must be tied to the actual configuration |

Keep a simple kitchen loading trial separate from a formal resistance report. If evidence covers only a silver fork but the proposed retail set also contains a decorated knife, narrow the wording or obtain evidence for the additional piece. If the lab result is conditional on particular care instructions, carry those conditions into the approved copy rather than shortening the claim for a package front.

The release gate is the buyer's written match between the claim, the tested samples and the final product revision. Until that match is documented, leave the claim off the artwork and listing; this guide supplies a request template, not a test result or a universal pass threshold.
