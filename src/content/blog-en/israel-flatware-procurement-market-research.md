*Research checked 12 November 2026. For large supermarket purchasing teams and e-commerce sales companies. The cover is an AI editorial illustration, not a factory photograph, tested sample or identifiable retail SKU.*

An Israel launch should start with a clearly specified family set and a separate replenishment offer, then test their economics by channel. The evidence supports comparing 24-piece sets, a 30-piece alternative and six-piece packs. It does not establish a national best seller or justify buying a deep assortment of colours. Hebrew information, set completeness and the distinction between adult and infant utensils belong in the purchase specification before price negotiation.

## Demand signals: a retail market with several different measures

CBS reported that chain-store sales at constant prices rose **2.6% in 2025**, following **7.2% in 2024**. This is a chain-retail indicator spanning food and non-food activities, not flatware sales or the whole retail market. The February 2026 release describes its figures as preliminary. It supports testing distribution through established chains; it does not provide a forecast for a new cutlery range. [CBS annual comparison](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/mediarelease/DocLib/2026/046/29_26_046b.pdf)

The CBS 2024 Social Survey table reports **58.9% of people aged 20 and over** using the internet for shopping during the three months preceding interview. The denominator is the adult population, not internet users, and the measure is participation, not expenditure or e-commerce's share of retail turnover. No verified national stainless-flatware online revenue series was obtained. A meaningful online buying audience and a particular product's sales potential are separate questions. [CBS table 17.16, page 2 and footnote 1](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/DocLib/2025/17.ShnatonScienceTechnologyCommunication/st17_16x.pdf)

The recent-news check included the CBS release directory, which lists 2026 chain-retail updates. Its underlying numerical release was not recovered in this research, so the article does not attach invented current-quarter growth to the annual figures. [CBS releases](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/Pages/SubjectPressReleases.aspx)

Figures are rounded to three decimals in millions; shares to one decimal.

HS8215 includes spoons, forks and other table or kitchen articles, with different metals and finishes. HS821191 covers fixed-blade table knives. Neither identifies a complete stainless-steel consumer market; the two headings should not be mechanically added to estimate a household-set market. Assorted sets need classification on their actual composition.

HS8215 import value fell **6.0% between 2023 and 2025**; the China-partner share moved from **84.8% to 74.7%**. Over the same period, HS821191 value rose **40.3%** from a smaller base. These different directions argue for checking the precise assortment and customs code before claiming that “cutlery demand” rose or fell. They do not prove changes in retail unit sales.

The API's HS8215 records and world knife totals are aggregates; the China knife rows are marked reported. Every available net-weight observation in this extract is marked estimated. World HS8215 net weight is missing for 2024 and 2025, and zero quantity fields do not mean zero imports. We therefore calculate neither pieces sold nor import price per piece.

CBS's commodity-download page describes its import partners as countries of origin, with gross, provisional data. Its broader methodological publication also explains a country-of-purchase presentation unless otherwise specified. These are not interchangeable denominators. The Comtrade partner labels above are retained as returned; the 12 cells have not been independently reconciled against a national-file extraction. A buyer should request origin evidence for the actual shipment rather than infer it from the partner share. [CBS commodity files](https://www.cbs.gov.il/en/Pages/importAndExport.aspx); [CBS methodology, partner-country definitions](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/doclib/2022/fr_trade10_2022/intro_e.pdf)

## What a supermarket buyer can test

A first range hypothesis is a plainly labelled **24-piece, six-place everyday set**, plus one visibly different upgrade. IKEA's 59, 95 and 175 ILS examples establish three observed price points, while Naaman's Bella at 99.90 shows promotional competition near the middle. They do not prove that an unknown brand can sustain any of these prices. Compare packaging, brand support, weight, finish and actual replacement terms before setting a shelf target.

MARTORP's 30-piece set adds dessert forks; it is not simply a 24-piece set with six spare teaspoons. Put the actual contents on the front of the box and on the shelf specification. For a supermarket planogram, request the supplier's retail-box width, height, depth, hanging or standing format, case quantity and barcode position. IKEA's four package records provide practical comparison points; they cannot be copied as the dimensions of a new supplier's product.

Keep coloured finishes as a controlled test. Camden specifies hand washing, and FOX's Glory explicitly excludes dishwashers; Harmony permits dishwasher use with prompt cleaning and drying. An upgraded appearance can therefore bring a different care obligation. Put any validated limitation in Hebrew alongside the product promise. Do not sell an untested coated set under an unrestricted dishwasher claim.

Choose pilot quantities from the chain's own comparable sell-through and shelf capacity. Track full-price and promotional sales separately, missing-piece complaints, damaged packaging and returns by finish. No public sample here supplies an evidence-based national opening order, lead time or guaranteed turnover.

## What an e-commerce company should cost separately

Six-piece Diner packs make a different replenishment proposition from complete family sets. At the observed FOX terms, a single teaspoon pack at 34.93 plus 19.90 delivery totals **54.83 ILS**; delivery is **57.0%** of the displayed item price. This is consumer checkout arithmetic under the published threshold, not the seller's carrier cost or a verified basket. It suggests testing multi-pack baskets or add-on sales rather than assuming a low item price alone produces a compelling delivered offer. [Diner offer and delivery terms](https://www.foxhome.co.il/products/0236799900)

Naaman advertises delivery within 14 business days, charging 30 ILS for orders up to 199 and free delivery above that amount. FOX advertises 6–8 business days, 19.90 ILS up to 249 and free delivery above 249, with possible peak-period delays. These are local retail policies, not an international supplier's transit commitments. [Naaman offer and delivery terms](https://www.naamanp.co.il/catalog/product/view/id/17932/s/11317828/category/1256/); [FOX offer and delivery terms](https://www.foxhome.co.il/products/0245809900)

Build a listing from the signed bill of materials: each utensil type and count, material claim supported by supplier documentation, actual finish, verified care, dimensions and package contents. Ask for a packed sample to check scratching, knife retention, count accuracy and parcel handling. Agree acceptance criteria with the buyer; this article does not present these checks as statutory certification tests.

Cost the sale with revenue excluding applicable tax, landed product cost, labelling, storage, pick-and-pack, payment or platform fees, delivery subsidy, advertising and an evidence-based return provision. Use the seller's own fee schedules and tax treatment. No wholesale price, freight quote, tax recovery entitlement or target margin is inferred from retail prices.

## Import, food-contact and Hebrew information: define the product first

The Israeli Tax Authority's live tariff, checked 12 November, distinguishes other assorted sets under **8215200000/4**, other articles under **8215990000/8**, and the knives branch **8211911000/3**. The checked entries show general customs exemption running from **1 June 2023 to 31 May 2028** and purchase-tax exemption. This is a dated tariff observation for those entries, not a binding classification, an exemption from VAT or a landed-cost quotation. The knife entry also displays an import-prohibition warning requiring product-specific review. [Sets entry](https://shaarolami-query.customs.mof.gov.il/CustomspilotWeb/he/CustomsBook/Import/ImportCustomsItemDetails?customsItemId=3244&isDiscountCode=False); [Other articles](https://shaarolami-query.customs.mof.gov.il/CustomspilotWeb/he/CustomsBook/Import/ImportCustomsItemDetails?customsItemId=8071&isDiscountCode=False); [Knives entry](https://shaarolami-query.customs.mof.gov.il/CustomspilotWeb/he/CustomsBook/Import/ImportCustomsItemDetails?customsItemId=62&isDiscountCode=False)

For HS8215, the official Free Import system identifies **cutlery and feeding articles for children up to 36 months**, with SI14372 declaration or approval requirements. That description must not be imposed automatically on every adult stainless set, nor used to declare every adult product exempt from all safety duties. Food-contact polymer or elastomer layers require a separate scope check; the decree lists SI5113 for specified food-contact products with such layers. A colour name alone establishes neither the coating chemistry nor a conformity route. [Official Free Import decree and product search](https://apps.economy.gov.il/Apps/FreeImport/decree)

The system's latest linked September 2026 amendments concern vehicle and aircraft-related headings, not the cutlery entries cited here. This comparison supports the narrow distinction above; it is not a blanket clearance for all materials or future imports. Obtain the importer or competent laboratory's written assessment for the actual adult/child use, alloy, coating and customs line before ordering. [23 September 2026 amendments](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/free_import_order/he/Hofshi_import-order-2200-230926.pdf)

Section 17 of the Consumer Protection Law sets out identification information including product name, country of manufacture, producer or importer identification and address, quantity and constituent materials; Hebrew is the default language unless otherwise provided. The statutory text hosted by the Consumer Council and the regulator's published explanation support that baseline. The regulator also explains translating foreign-language use instructions and warnings into Hebrew, and the precedence of conflicting product-specific legislation. These sources do not approve a finished label or every possible packaging exception. [Statutory text, page 19](https://www.consumers.org.il/files/files/madrih-simun-mazon/hakika/hok1.pdf); [Regulator's labelling explanation](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/cpfta_display_of_prices)

For the purchase file, obtain the actual composition and coating specification, intended age group, classification decision, applicable test or declaration documents, importer details, origin evidence and approved Hebrew artwork. Treat these as product-specific release conditions. This research does not certify the sampled retailers' compliance or claim that a generated image represents tested goods.

## A usable enquiry for the first purchase

Send two briefs: a shelf-ready family set and an online replenishment pack. For each, request confirmed contents, material evidence, care validation, packaging dimensions and weights, case quantity, minimum order, production lead time, replacement-piece policy and the documents needed by the Israeli importer. Compare both offers against a local delivered or shelf price after your own costs, then authorize a measured pilot.
