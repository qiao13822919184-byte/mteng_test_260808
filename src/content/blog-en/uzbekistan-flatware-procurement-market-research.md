For Uzbekistan, the most defensible starting point is a **small, clearly specified household range with a local replenishment plan**. Public offers support testing different set contents and presentation levels. They do not establish how many sets a new supplier can sell, or whether an advertised discount will survive a wholesale ordering cycle.

Supermarket buyers should compare usable place settings, the shelf package and the local distributor's available stock. E-commerce sales companies should compare the same contents against the actual cash price, delivery conditions and care instructions. A low advertised price on an unavailable product is not an executable buying benchmark.

Research date: **31 October–1 November 2026, Asia/Shanghai**. This is public-source desk research, without store visits, checkout purchases, interviews or testing. Prices below are **Uzbekistani soums (UZS)**; import values are current US dollars. The cover is an AI-generated illustration, not an actual retailer SKU, factory photograph or test record.

## 1. Read the national channel data as separate series

The National Statistics Committee's SIAT tables provide the following annual series, both in **billion UZS**. Retail turnover covers the overall consumer market; the e-commerce indicator draws on enterprise reporting and trade surveys. Neither isolates stainless-steel flatware.

|---|---:|---:|
| 2023 | 330,448.1 | 13,263.8 |
| 2024 | 407,416.0 | 15,211.5 |
| 2025 | 481,234.5 | 31,551.9 |

The retail table was updated on 23 October 2026; the e-commerce table on 10 September 2026. The latter still carries a note referring to 2016–2024 although a 2025 column is present. Preserve the retrieval date and table version in planning documents. [SIAT retail turnover](https://siat.stat.uz/data/2704/?lang=en), [SIAT e-commerce volume](https://siat.stat.uz/data/2833/?lang=en).

A separate National Agency of Perspective Projects announcement reports **22.7 trillion UZS** in 2025 electronic commerce, a **4.6%** retail share, and **115** platforms compared with **36** previously. That total differs from SIAT's 31.5519 trillion UZS. This research did not reconcile their coverage or revision history. Do not combine the agency's share with SIAT's total, average the totals, or convert either into a cutlery sales forecast. [NAPP original announcement](https://t.me/napp_uz/1831).

The practical conclusion is limited but useful: online distribution deserves a separate commercial plan. Its viability for a particular flatware set still depends on local sell-through, settlement, returns and delivery costs, which these national totals do not provide.

## 5. Separate tariff classification, declarations and consumer information

Uzbekistan-specific requirements must be checked for the actual product and shipment. The current PP-3818 tariff table displays **15% for heading 8215** and **5% for heading 8211**. These are classification reference points, not a landed-cost quotation. Confirm the national code, set classification, origin treatment, customs value and other applicable charges with the importer before costing. Do not assign every mixed set the rate for one individual utensil. [PP-3818 tariff text](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3802366).

In Resolution 43's current Annex 4, item **100**, heading **8215**, appears under the section for products requiring a **declaration of conformity**, rather than the earlier certificate section. The displayed amendment is Resolution 472 of 7 September 2026. The page separately flags changes effective 1 January 2027; they are not treated as already effective. Confirm which technical regulation, standards and sanitary requirements apply to the actual steel, coating and handle materials. The listing alone is not proof that a shipment complies, and the route for separately classified knives must be checked independently. [Resolution 43, current Uzbek text](https://lex.uz/docs/5249376).

The consumer-protection law's Article 6 requires necessary, truthful product information in the state language, with other languages additionally permitted, including relevant product properties, price and manufacturer/importer information. Article 28¹ also addresses information before an electronic sale. Prepare Uzbek consumer information and match it to the listing; a Russian-only catalogue is not evidence that an importer has satisfied its obligations. [Consumer-protection law](https://lex.uz/ru/acts/14643).

PP-3818 distinguishes customs-release labelling from specified conformity/sanitary-document conditions. Its customs-stage wording must not be read as a blanket exemption from consumer information requirements. The precise product-specific labelling and food-contact test route remains a pre-order confirmation item; this research has not established a universal migration limit, dishwasher qualification or safety certification for the sampled products.

A timely cost distinction matters for online sellers: PP-368, paragraph 19, reduces the existing customs-clearance fee rate for **international courier consignments by 20% from 1 November 2026**. This concerns that fee, not a 20% reduction in flatware import duty, total freight or an ordinary bulk import's landed cost. Obtain an updated quotation from the courier for a qualifying shipment. [PP-368, paragraph 19](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/8536690).

## 6. Turn the evidence into separate channel trials

The following are purchasing hypotheses to validate, not measured consumer preferences.

| Decision | Large physical retailer / supermarket | E-commerce sales company |
|---|---|---|
| First assortment | Test a clearly counted 18- or 24-piece family set; compare functionality before piece count | Test a compact complete set with contents visible in the first product images |
| Price reference | Obtain a stable distributor quote; compare against the available promotional examples, with replenishment terms | Use actual checkout and settlement, not a crossed-out price or monthly instalment |
| Larger sets | Add a limited gift line only after measured sell-through and space economics | Keep large gift sets separate from the entry offer; price protective packing and returns |
| Packaging | Request actual box dimensions, shelf presentation, barcode and inner/master-carton quantities | Request packed dimensions, weight, separators and parcel-damage evidence |
| Information | Check Uzbek information, importer identification, exact contents and care instructions | Make listing, pack, barcode and care restrictions agree; do not add unproven 18/10 or dishwasher claims |
| Replenishment | Ask for local stock by SKU, split deliveries, minimum reorder and measured lead-time range | Monitor available stock and orders separately; do not advertise unavailable variants as ready to ship |

For a shelf trial, record units sold per store per week, days genuinely in stock, display space, markdowns and missing-piece returns. For an online trial, record delivered orders, cancellations, return reasons, payment settlement, parcel cost and replacement cost. Neither channel should treat out-of-stock days as evidence of weak demand or displayed stock as a reliable sales-rate proxy.

Define the reorder point from **observed demand during the measured replenishment lead time plus an explicit safety stock**. A local distributor may shorten the retailer's replenishment cycle, but the available public pages do not establish its inbound lead time, wholesale MOQ or credit terms. Ask for dated quotations and a realistic split-delivery schedule instead of assuming a fixed number of days.

## 7. What must be resolved before committing inventory

Reconfirm stock and fulfilment at the intended branches of both sampled chains before placing an order; the Mediapark evidence remains historical. Recheck the four unavailable catalogue entries before using them as active competitors. Obtain an independently reconciled national commodity series if the decision depends on the size or growth of imports, and clarify the two official e-commerce series before using a market-share assumption.

For each shortlisted SKU, collect a signed contents list, steel and coating specifications, applicable conformity and food-contact documents, label artwork, retail and shipping dimensions, actual supply availability, wholesale terms and a destination-specific cost quotation. Obtain buyer approval for an actual sample before scaling a trial; no sampled merchant claim here establishes that another supplier's product has the same material or performance.

Uzbekistan's public evidence supports **testing a small, accurately described range and measuring replenishment economics**. It does not yet support a nationwide volume commitment, a universal entry price, or a guaranteed margin. The unresolved stock, fulfilment and data gaps should be closed before turning this research into a buying budget.
