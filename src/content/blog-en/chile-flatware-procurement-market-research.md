*Research checked 16 November 2026. For large supermarket procurement teams and e-commerce sales companies. Cover: AI editorial illustration, not an actual product, factory photograph or test.*

Chile supports a useful sourcing comparison between compact everyday sets, larger family sets and higher-priced finishes. The immediate buying decision is which complete set can meet a channel's price and fulfilment requirements after its contents, packaging and seller conditions have been checked. Twelve public product offers below provide benchmarks for that decision; they do not establish a national average price or prove demand for a new imported range.

## 1. Read retail growth and online growth on their own terms

[INE's September 2026 release](https://www.ine.gob.cl/sala-de-prensa/prensa/general/noticia/2026/10/30/%C3%ADndice-de-actividad-del-comercio-anot%C3%B3-una-variaci%C3%B3n-de-2-7-interanualmente-en-septiembre-de-2026) reports that retail trade excluding motor vehicles grew 4.1% year on year at constant prices. Supermarket sales rose 0.4% on the same constant-price basis. The retail e-commerce index increased 20.2% year on year at current prices, with cumulative growth of 15.8%. The online index's leading product line combined electronics, home equipment and technology. None of these figures isolates flatware.

The distinction matters: comparing nominal online growth with real supermarket growth does not measure how much flatware demand has moved online. Nor does an index growth rate give a total market value. For a supermarket, the evidence supports testing a manageable assortment with measured shelf turnover. For an online operator, it supports investigating digital execution, without setting a flatware sales forecast from the headline percentage.

The recent-news check also found [INE's 13 November inventory release](https://www.ine.gob.cl/sala-de-prensa/prensa/general/noticia/2026/11/13/%C3%ADndice-de-inventarios-del-comercio-aument%C3%B3-2-4-en-septiembre-de-2026): September commercial inventories rose 2.4% from August, with retail contributing 1.296 percentage points. This is broad inventory context, not evidence of a cutlery shortage. A buyer should ask for actual category sell-through and stock age before increasing order depth.

The national [current tariff page](https://www.aduana.cl/arancel-aduanero-vigente/aduana/2016-12-30/090118.html) and its [Section XV workbook](https://www.aduana.cl/aduana/site/docs/20161230/20161230090118/descarga_seccion_xv.xlsx) list 8211.9100 for those knives, 8215.2000 for other sets, and 8215.9900 for other articles; precious-metal-plated categories are separate. Chapter 82's notes govern mixed assortments. A buyer should classify the actual combination rather than assume every retail set belongs in one code. These rows are research categories, not a customs ruling for a supplier's SKU.

HS8215 import value increased from USD15.697 million to USD19.821 million over the period. But its 2025 net weight was slightly below 2024, so value growth alone cannot establish unit growth. China supplied 89.5% of 2025 value in this heading; that is a sourcing concentration indicator, not a consumer preference survey. Domestic production, retail margins, stock changes and material composition are absent from these import totals.

The [official Aduanas DIN catalogue](https://datos.gob.cl/api/3/action/package_show?id=385fb63b-c898-4690-aa3f-d59d933fc30c) supplies monthly declaration-item archives, while its [data dictionary](https://datos.gob.cl/dataset/385fb63b-c898-4690-aa3f-d59d933fc30c/resource/b40ec19e-a944-40d0-a38a-bb950188004d/download/descripcion-y-estructura-de-datos-.xlsx) separates declaration-level CIF from item-level CIF_ITEM. We checked that structure and the national tariff, but have not independently rebuilt and reconciled every monthly national declaration against this three-year Comtrade series. That reconciliation remains open. The saved Comtrade records retain quantity fields and estimation flags: HS821191 net weights are estimated, so they are unsuitable for deriving precise weight per knife. No stainless-only market size or national piece-sales total is claimed.

## 4. Supermarket buying: compare usable place settings and shelf space

The same headline piece count can describe different dinner services. FÖRNUFT and IKEA 365+ 24-piece sets each list six knives, six forks, six spoons and six teaspoons. MOPSIG's 16 pieces provide four of each. MARTORP's 30 pieces add six dessert forks to a six-person configuration. The 56-piece IKEA 365+ lists eight of seven utensil types, including dessert knives, forks and spoons. Do not present it as fourteen standard four-piece place settings.

For a supermarket pilot, a reasonable hypothesis from this sample is one compact everyday set and one clearly differentiated step-up set. FÖRNUFT at CLP7990 in promotion, Camden at CLP13990 and Ovalados at CLP18490 form useful 24-piece reference points, but they are not interchangeable specifications. The step-up must earn its shelf position through documented thickness, balance, finish, per-piece mass or packaging presentation, rather than an unsupported stainless grade claim.

Packaging changes the replenishment decision. MOPSIG's stated retail pack is 5×4×21 cm and 0.50 kg; the 56-piece IKEA 365+ is 26×6×29 cm and 2.91 kg. These are individual retail packages, not wholesale cartons. Ask for a filled shelf trial, safe knife retention, barcode readability, a Spanish contents list, units per case and measured case weight before approving the store allocation.

Set the first quantity from the retailer's own comparable-SKU sales and shelf capacity. As a planning method, units needed equal expected weekly sales multiplied by replenishment lead time, plus an agreed safety stock, less usable stock and confirmed inbound stock. Lead time must include import clearance and domestic distribution. Neither the public offers nor the import figures reveal those inputs; an invented national MOQ or automatic two-month opening order would be unjustified.

## 5. E-commerce buying: price the delivered order and the dispatch model

The [IKEA delivery page](https://www.ikea.com/cl/es/customer-service/services/delivery/) describes mainland coverage but also lists exclusions and warns of possible regional delays. It says service options and charges depend on destination commune, order size and current logistics availability. Its store-pickup option is therefore a separate choice to test, not proof that every item can be delivered everywhere at one rate.

[Paris Marketplace's April 2026 seller terms](https://ayuda.marketplace.paris.cl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/TyC-2026-04.pdf) distinguish logistics arrangements and a fulfilment inventory flow, and allow delivery coverage to be restricted for operational reasons. Seller identity, stock availability and dispatch responsibility need to be recorded together. UnionX's Cerdeña offer is not evidence that Paris owns that stock, and the presence of marketplace fulfilment services does not prove this particular seller uses them. Obtain the seller agreement and SKU-level dispatch method before pricing a listing.

Use a second outer parcel around the retail pack where needed for handling and knife security. Verify the packed order's dimensions and mass with the carrier; the IKEA retail dimensions above are only a starting point. Ask separately for collection or drop-off arrangements, cut-off times, regional charges, reverse-logistics charges and damage liability. No checkout address was submitted in this research, so no postcode-specific fee or delivery date is asserted.

For a transparent price sensitivity example, take a CLP19990 retail price assumed to include 19% VAT. Revenue before VAT would be approximately CLP16798. If a buyer's *hypothetical* combined platform, payment, dispatch and returns allowance were CLP5000 and its required contribution CLP3000, the remaining ceiling for landed merchandise would be CLP8798. Those allowances are not quoted platform rates. Recalculate using the actual tax treatment, fee bases, delivery subsidy and returns experience; a cheap set can become uneconomic as a single parcel.

List every utensil type and quantity in text, show the actual finish and relevant measurements, identify the seller, and expose delivery charges before purchase. A compact 16-piece trial and a distinct 24-piece upgrade are more informative experiments than several near-identical listings with unverified material claims. Test conversion, contribution after returns and fulfilment performance before extending colour or piece-count variants.

## 6. Imports, Spanish information and food contact: build a product-specific file

The official Section XV tariff workbook lists a general 6% rate for the cited national knife and flatware rows. [Aduanas' tax explanation](https://www.aduana.cl/cuanto-impuesto-se-paga-al-importar/aduana/2022-06-29/121230.html) illustrates the general calculation: CIF USD1000, duty USD60, and VAT at 19% of USD1060, or USD201.40. A valid trade preference can change duty; neither China origin nor a retail page alone establishes entitlement. Have the importer confirm classification, origin documentation, applicable preferences and any additional requirements for the actual shipment. Do not apply a low-value consumer-parcel regime automatically to commercial stock replenishment, or treat recoverable VAT as a final margin cost without checking the buyer's position.

[Chile's consolidated consumer law, articles 32–33](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=1160403), requires understandable Spanish basic commercial information and substantiable product and advertising statements. Prepare Spanish identity, contents, care and warranty information consistently across the retail pack and online record. Calling a material 18/10, a finish dishwasher safe, or a coating durable requires evidence for the supplied product; another retailer's wording is not that evidence.

The [electronic-commerce regulation, Decree 6](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=1165504), articles 6–13, covers Spanish information, seller and platform roles, essential product characteristics, total cost, availability and delivery or collection information. These are requirements for the buyer's downstream consumer offer. They do not convert its wholesale supply contract into an ordinary consumer purchase.

The [Sanitary Food Regulation, Decree 977](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=71271&idVersion=2026-10-02), article 122(h), expressly includes domestic cutlery in its utensil definition. Article 123 addresses suitable materials and avoidance of harmful transfer; article 125 sets requirements for metals in food contact. Build a materials-and-finish specification and agree a relevant compliance assessment with the importer and competent laboratory. Do not translate the article's wording about impurities into a simplistic prohibition on the intentional chromium or iron content of stainless steel, or advertise a generic “food grade” certificate as proof of compliance. Coloured finishes require their own documented composition and assessment. This research verifies the applicable source provisions; it does not certify any of the twelve sampled products or a future supplier SKU.

## 7. A two-channel RFQ that can lead to an order decision

Send suppliers a common technical sheet containing utensil-by-utensil counts, alloy and finish declarations, dimensions, mass, edge-finish criteria, care instructions, test documents tied to the exact construction, and approved Spanish artwork. Ask for photographs and samples of the actual proposed SKU; the cover illustration is not a product specification.

For supermarket quotes, request the retail display dimensions, unit and master-case barcodes, case quantity, pallet configuration, delivery to the retailer's distribution centre, replenishment terms and a measured shelf trial. For online quotes, add the parcel-ready outer pack, transport testing appropriate to the route, product-data fields, fulfilment integration, regional delivery matrix and returns responsibility. Quote both versions against the same agreed utensil specification so packaging and logistics differences are visible.

Advance a pilot only after its landed-cost sheet, material evidence, Spanish information and channel-specific packaging have been reviewed. Keep checkout delivery charges, current stock, future promotional prices, national-data reconciliation and SKU-specific customs treatment on the open-questions register until verified.
