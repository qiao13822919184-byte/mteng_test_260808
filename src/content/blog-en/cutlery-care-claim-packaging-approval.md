“Dishwasher safe” is short enough to fit on a retail box, but it is still a product claim. A private-label buyer should not copy it from a competing set or apply it to every knife, fork and spoon because the pieces look alike. Before artwork is printed, compare the proposed wording with the **exact component construction, finish and intended cleaning conditions**. This is an editorial approval workflow, not a claim that any Yumingxing model has passed a dishwasher test.

Yumingxing's [current catalogue guidance](/en/) says material choices and finishes vary by model and component, and asks buyers to confirm the specification. Independent cutlery testing organization [CATRA describes dishwasher-resistance evaluation](https://www.catra.org/testing-services/testing-product-evaluation/) among available product tests. The existence of a test method does not prove that a particular supplier's product was tested or passed.

## Give every care statement an owner and a basis

| Artwork field | Approval question | Record to keep |
|---|---|---|
| Product identity | Does this text apply to the exact set and revision being packed? | Model, component list and artwork revision. |
| Materials and finish | Do all pieces share the relevant construction, coating or handle treatment? | Component-by-component specification. |
| Cleaning instruction | Is the proposed hand-wash or machine-wash wording supported for each piece and the stated conditions? | Supplier response and any product-specific evaluation requested by the buyer. |
| Limitations | Are there conditions or exclusions that the label must explain? | Approved wording and decision owner. |
| Language and placement | Can the target-market buyer read the care instruction on the final pack or insert? | Final language proof and pack mockup. |

Mark a line **approved**, **needs evidence**, or **remove from artwork**. An unfilled cell is not an approval. If a set combines different components, ask whether one general instruction covers every piece; otherwise make the wording piece-specific or withhold the unsupported claim. Keep the source file and print proof together so a later design revision does not restore old wording.

The [RFQ checklist](/en/blog/what-to-include-in-a-cutlery-rfq/) helps identify what is actually in the set, while the [retail-box and shipping-carton brief](/en/blog/cutlery-retail-packaging-shipping-carton-brief/) separates consumer-facing text from transport marks. Ask Yumingxing through the [enquiry page](/en/contact/) which instructions and supporting records apply to the proposed model. Confirm any destination-specific label obligations with the buyer's qualified local reviewer; this article does not prescribe a country's legal wording.
