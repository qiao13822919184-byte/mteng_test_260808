For a Spain flatware launch, approve the **number of usable place settings, the exact sales channel and the packaged unit** before comparing prices. A 60-piece pack can have a different household function from a 56-piece pack. A store promotion does not automatically set the online selling price. These distinctions determine what a supermarket should put on a shelf and what an e-commerce company can profitably send in one parcel.

Research accessed **10 November 2026, Asia/Shanghai**. The main sample contains **13 publicly offered SKUs** from IKEA Spain and Carrefour Spain, two physical retail organisations with two overlapping own online channels, not four independent retailers. IKEA's store network and Carrefour's named store availability establish the physical-channel references; there was no shop-floor audit. Consumer prices below are **EUR**, before delivery and unconfirmed basket discounts. No postcode checkout, consumer survey, wholesale quotation or laboratory test was conducted. The cover is an **AI-generated editorial illustration**, not an actual factory, SKU, shipment or test. [IKEA Spain stores](https://www.ikea.com/es/es/stores/).

## 1. Read retail and e-commerce indicators on their own terms

INE's provisional September 2026 retail index, released on **29 October**, fell **1.4% year on year** and **0.4% month on month** after seasonal and calendar adjustment, at constant prices. The unadjusted annual figure was **−1.6%**. These are broad retail-volume indicators, not flatware revenue. [INE September retail release](https://www.ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/ICM0926.htm).

CNMC reports that its measured 2025 e-commerce turnover exceeded **EUR114.8 billion**, up **20.6%**; the latest quarterly release identified in this review puts **Q1 2026 at EUR29.643 billion**, up **15.1%**. The coverage includes services and cross-border transactions. Its methodology follows eligible bank-card payment transactions with Spain as origin and/or destination; it does not capture every payment method. Do not compare these nominal transaction values directly with INE's real retail index, or call either a stainless-flatware market estimate. [CNMC annual release](https://cnmc.es/prensa/comercio-electronico-20260703); [CNMC Q1 release](https://www.cnmc.es/prensa/comercio-electronico-20261002); [CNMC methodology](https://data.cnmc.es/sites/default/files/2024-11/Nota%20metodol%C3%B3gica%20comercio%20electr%C3%B3nico.pdf).

Our buying interpretation is to test a tightly defined household proposition, rather than extrapolate broad online growth into a national cutlery sales forecast. Ask the supermarket for category sell-through and shelf productivity; ask the online business for conversion, returned units and contribution after fulfilment. Those missing category measures matter more to the first order than national transaction growth alone.

## 4. Compare the dining function, then compare the offer

For a basic four-piece setting, count one knife, fork, spoon and teaspoon. The smallest count among those four types determines complete settings; accessories and a storage tray do not add diners. This is our purchasing calculation, not a retailer sales metric.

| Comparison | Verified arithmetic | Buying implication |
|---|---|---|
| FÖRNUFT 24 | EUR9.99 / 6 = EUR1.67 per four-piece setting | Entry reference before delivery; not a wholesale target |
| DRAGON 24 online | EUR19.99 / 6 = EUR3.33 per setting | Keep web price separate from store campaign |
| DRAGON store campaign | EUR14.99 / 6 = EUR2.50 per setting | Source says store only, 01.09.26–31.12.26; verify chosen store |
| MARTORP 30 | EUR39.99 / 6 = EUR6.67 per five-piece setting | Dessert forks add function, not extra diners |
| Carrefour 60 + tray | EUR19.99 / 15 = EUR1.33 per four-piece setting | Tray is additional; do not compare blindly with 56 pieces |

IKEA's 56-piece set supplies **8 of 7 types**, so it serves eight diners with dessert utensils; Carrefour's 60-piece set supplies **15 of 4 types**. Neither is inherently the better household offer. The former adds a dessert service, the latter more complete basic settings. Do not infer a typical Spanish household size from either pack. PRAKTBOTIA and the polypropylene-handled Carrefour set still need per-type counts before setting-based comparison.

MOPSIG's **EUR4.99** is a dated promotion against a stated former **EUR6.99**; it is not a permanent entry-price promise. DRAGON's **EUR14.99** store campaign is below its **EUR19.99** web offer. A buyer benchmarking online economics against the lower shelf figure would build a misleading margin plan. Keep offer date, membership conditions, location, tax treatment and delivery separate in the buying sheet.

## 5. Supermarket assortment: test a basic pack and a clear upgrade

A practical test, inferred from this sample, is a basic all-steel four-type household pack alongside one upgrade whose function is visible: additional dessert forks or clearly identified steak knives. The sample supports this comparison because FÖRNUFT and DRAGON use the same six-setting structure, MARTORP adds dessert forks, and New Forgeron/Versailles specify steak knives. It does not establish national demand shares or justify a prescribed order volume.

Approve a physical sample and a contents diagram for each selling unit. Confirm utensil counts, lengths, piece weight, edge finish, handle construction and the actual knife style before agreeing a shelf ticket. The polypropylene-handled EUR9.99 offer is a different construction from an all-steel EUR9.99 offer, even if both titles say stainless. Ask for separate care instructions and material evidence rather than copying one common benefit statement across both.

Use the intended shelf fixture to test facing width, hanging or standing stability, legibility and restocking. A clear presentation box can aid contents inspection, but its material and dimensions must come from the actual supplier, not from an unrelated retail sample. Ask for units per inner carton and master carton, barcode placement, replacement-piece supply, minimum order and reorder lead time. These were not available in the public samples. Start with a category-defined trial and expand only after measured sell-through, damage and missing-piece claims.

## 6. E-commerce selection: the retail pack is not the shipping parcel

The published FÖRNUFT 24 package is **23×13×4 cm / 0.95 kg**, while IKEA 365+ 56 is **29×26×6 cm / 2.91 kg**. Their simple bounding-box volumes are **1.196 L** and **4.524 L**, before any outer carton or cushioning. This comparison flags different handling needs; it does not supply a courier charge, dimensional-weight divisor or measured drop-test result.

Cost the actual shipped parcel. Request packed external dimensions, gross weight, restraint against utensil movement, knife-tip protection and packing photographs. Agree a transit test appropriate to the carrier and route; keep a signed contents/packaging reference for returns. Carrefour's 60-piece offer recommends hand washing, while the IKEA offers state dishwasher suitability. Treat those as seller claims for those products, not proof that another supplier's set can use identical care copy.

For a commercial test, calculate **net product revenue plus delivery collected, minus landed product cost, platform/payment fees, outer packaging, picking, shipping and expected returns**. Use the business's actual fees and tax position. The **EUR2.29 four-teaspoon** sample is a plausible replacement or basket add-on hypothesis; it is not proof of profitable standalone shipping. A large pack may spread shipping cost across more pieces but can increase damage or incomplete-return costs. Compare contribution per fulfilled order, not just retail price per utensil.

Show the real contents beside the item count in listing images and text, including whether a tray is extra. Maintain one SKU record across the page, warehouse pick list and returns checklist. Separate mainland and island/postcode delivery conditions; this research uses the main Spain site and does not transfer distinct island-site offers or rates into a national price claim.

## 7. Import, Spanish information and food-contact evidence

The following is a source-based buying checklist, not a declaration that any sampled SKU complies. The exact product, materials, intended contact and legal operator roles still need review before the order.

| Issue | Verified source boundary | Procurement action |
|---|---|---|
| Customs | EORI identifies customs operators; TARIC queries depend on classification, origin and date | Identify importer and confirm classification/rates for the actual set and knife construction |
| Food contact | EC1935/2004 governs safety and traceability; EC2023/2006 requires GMP | Obtain material identity, intended-use evidence and a traceable technical file; generic “stainless” is insufficient |
| Language and online offer | Spanish consumer law Art18 requires mandatory information at least in Castilian, subject to exceptions; GPSR Art19 covers distance-offer identification/contact/safety information within its scope | Approve Spanish label and listing together, with actual operator details and supported care statements |
| Packaging | Spain RD1055/2022 and EU2025/40 require role- and date-specific review | Allocate packaging registration/reporting responsibilities and collect packaging material/weight data |

Sources: [AEAT EORI](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/EORI.html); [AEAT TARIC help](https://www3.agenciatributaria.gob.es/static_files/common/internet/dep/aduanas/aytaric.html); [AESAN food-contact overview](https://www.aesan.gob.es/seguridad-alimentaria/materiales-contacto-alimentos/); [EC1935/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:02004R1935-20210327); [GMP regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:02006R2023-20250316); [Spanish consumer law](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-20555); [GPSR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:32023R0988); [Spain packaging regulation](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-22690); [EU packaging regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj/eng).

Do not revive **RD397/1990**: BOE marks it repealed with effect from **11 December 2020**. Do not transplant a French composition rule into a Spain compliance statement. AESAN distinguishes the general food-contact framework from material-specific measures. Under EC1935/2004 Art16, the declaration obligation depends on specific measures and possible national provisions; this article does not invent a universal stainless-steel certificate. Article15 also exempts clearly food-contact articles from the particular food-contact indication in paragraph1(a), without removing other applicable information duties. [BOE repeal notice](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1990-7739); [AESAN legislation index](https://www.aesan.gob.es/legislacion/legislacion-materiales-en-contacto).

The EU packaging regulation generally applies from **12 August 2026**, with specific provisions on other timetables. Do not describe all future harmonised labels as already compulsory, or treat the Spanish packaging text as the only relevant rule. Establish which entity first places the packaged product on the market and which packaging layers it supplies. Agree who provides registration and reporting data before print approval. For mixed handles, coatings or decorative finishes, obtain product-specific applicability and testing advice; these samples do not establish their chemistry or compliance.

## 8. A buying brief that can be answered with evidence

Send suppliers a structured request: target setting count; quantities by utensil type; steel/handle/finish identification; knife design; dimensions and weights; Spanish information; supporting food-contact documentation; selling-pack and shipping-pack drawings; carton quantities; and replacement/claim arrangements. Ask commercial terms separately: quotations at actual order levels, lead times, delivery basis and payment conditions. None is inferred from a consumer product page.

For the supermarket, the decision is whether the basic pack and the functional upgrade earn their shelf space under a measured trial. For the online company, it is whether each precise combination and parcel earns positive contribution after real fulfilment and returns. Require a revision-controlled specification shared by both channels, while allowing different outer packaging and channel-specific offers.

The remaining evidence gaps are category sales, actual local availability, final checkout cost, verified steel grades, per-type counts on ambiguous listings, shipping-carton data and supplier compliance files. DataComex reconciliation also remains open. These limits constrain the recommendations: this is a documented assortment and sourcing test, not a market-share forecast, a verified compliance certificate or a commitment to any named retailer's future price.
