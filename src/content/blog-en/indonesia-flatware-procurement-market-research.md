Indonesia's public flatware assortment supports testing **replacement multipacks, clearly itemised family sets and portable kits as separate buying propositions**. Headline piece counts cannot make two sets comparable, and an 18/10 claim does not prove Indonesian conformity.

Supermarket buyers need a shelf proposition and reliable replenishment. E-commerce sales companies need delivered contents, product information and fulfilment costs that support the offer. The following research turns those requirements into separate procurement briefs.

Product/trade research: **5 November 2026, Asia/Shanghai**; regulatory clarification: **6 November 2026**. Public-source desk research only: no store visits, checkout purchases, interviews or laboratory tests. Prices are **Indonesian rupiah (IDR)**; import values are current USD. The cover is an **AI-generated editorial illustration**, not a retailer SKU, factory photograph or test record.

## 1. National demand context: keep the measures separate

Bank Indonesia reported August 2026 real retail sales growth of **1.3% year on year**. Its 9 October release projected September growth of **1.4%**, with a **2.3% monthly decline**. September figures in that release are forecasts, not realised results. The overall index provides channel context, not flatware demand. [Bank Indonesia retail survey release](https://www.bi.go.id/en/publikasi/ruang-media/news-release/Pages/sp_2821726.aspx).

The Ministry of Trade's *PMSE Indonesia 2025* report, reproducing BPS survey figures for **2024**, records e-commerce business revenue of **IDR 1,288.93 trillion**, up **17.08%**. Marketplaces accounted for **15.79%**, non-marketplace methods **84.21%**. These numbers come from the ministry's presentation of BPS results; the BPS publication landing page was located, but its complete PDF could not be downloaded. BPS defines the indicator as income from online sales, including both methods—not profit, marketplace GMV or flatware sales. [Ministry report, PDF page 10](https://bkperdag.kemendag.go.id/unduhan-file/a11ba920-123c-4a4f-856b-cdbb1f0f64c4); [BPS definition](https://sirusa.web.bps.go.id/metadata/indikator/71aefa49-9267-fbcc-94bb-b001898070e2).

**Inference for buyers:** evaluate retailers' own shops as well as platforms. No verified flatware category share or national consumer survey was obtained; multiplying national e-commerce revenue by an assumed share would not produce a defensible order forecast.

## 4. Compare function and contents before price per piece

**Replacement packs:** TANICA DH's six dinner spoons and six dinner forks were each IDR 19,900, with SS410 and 1.0 mm in the titles. NATUR's six cake forks were IDR 49,900, versus IDR 59,900 for six dinner forks. These differences are not a controlled material-price experiment. The phrase “Prime Grade Stainless Steel” does not establish a specific certified grade.

**Portable kits:** Pocky, silver and maroon offers were IDR 53,910, 55,920 and 63,920. All list one spoon, one fork, one pair of chopsticks and a case. Silver says three pieces; maroon says four, while its commodity name ends in “(3)”. The physical contents, case construction and grade evidence matter more than dividing by that headline count. These offers justify a segment test, not a market-size claim.

**Family sets:** TANICA's two 20-piece sets were IDR 179,000 and 199,000, with store stock but online sell-outs. Their complete component breakdown was not established from captured text. Request the contents list before calling either a five-person or knife-inclusive set.

Culinart 1022, SKU 10610463, has a material conflict: **18/10** in specifications, **SS430** in its commodity name. Obtain a corrected record and actual material evidence; do not choose the more attractive description. Implausible temperature fields on other listings were excluded from analysis.

## 6. Import and conformity decisions before artwork approval

Industry Regulation **53/2024**, Article 2, mandates **SNI 8753:2020** for covered stainless flatware, including imports. The listed scope includes 8211.10.00, 8211.91.00, ex 8211.92.99 and relevant 8215 subheadings. Article 6 restricts Type 4 certification to specified small-industry/foreign micro-small conditions; buyers cannot freely pick the cheapest scheme. Verify manufacturer, product scope, representative and certification body.

Annex II requires product SNI marking and an electronic mark on the smallest pack after SPPT SNI approval, plus manufacturer, brand/model, production-code and origin information. Import marking occurs at the overseas factory. [53/2024 original, Articles 2, 6 and Annex II](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/368398/2024pmperin053.pdf).

“Food grade”, 304 and dishwasher-safe attributes do not prove conformity. Ask the designated certification body for applicable clauses and a report covering the actual item, including coatings/plastic components. The complete paid standard and product-specific test reports were not obtained here; **no migration limits or sample certificates are invented**.

For commercial resale, verify the importer and its NIB/API under **16/2025 as amended by 37/2025 and 18/2026**. Article 7 distinguishes API-U resale from API-P production use. The ministry confirms the latest amendment's promulgation on 4 June 2026. Consignment-specific classification, restrictions, duty, origin preference and tax treatment require a current importer/customs check before ordering. This article offers no zero-duty or automatic-permission conclusion. [16/2025](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/384921/Permendag%20Nomor%2016%20tahun%202025.pdf), [37/2025](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/395126/Permendag%20Nomor%2037%20Tahun%202025.pdf), [18/2026](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/351566/permendag-no-18-tahun-2026), [ministry implementation explanation](https://www.kemendag.go.id/berita/siaran-pers/kemendag-sosialisasikan-permendag-nomor-18-tahun-2026-tingkatkan-efektivitas-layanan-perizinan-impor-dan-kepastian-hukum).

Do not infer scope from a short title: 23/2025's Article 2 lists specific consumer-goods categories, not every consumer product. The tableware row in 25/2021 includes ceramic/glass descriptions and is not a ready-made steel-cutlery label checklist. Prepare Indonesian consumer information for buyer review while mapping the actual product to legal requirements. [23/2025](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/330402/permendag-no-23-tahun-2025); [25/2021 tableware row](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/269197/Permendag%20Nomor%2025%20Tahun%202021%20%281%29.pdf).

**SNI certification and NPB registration have different scope tests.** Regulation **33/2025**, Annex II G, obligation 2(b)–(c) (PDF page 95), ties mandatory NPB registration to Annex III B and explicitly states that mandatory-SNI goods not yet in that list are not yet required to hold NPB. Review of Annex III B (PDF pages 163–260) found melamine and ceramic tableware entries, but no stainless-flatware entry under HS 8211/8215. The official database marks 33/2025 in force and the current ministry service still refers to that list.

**Our reading of those sources:** do not automatically add an NPB requirement merely because SNI 8753 applies. This does not remove the separate compulsory SNI duties in 53/2024. Before a specific shipment, have the importer check its exact item, HS code and any effective list changes; this research is not a shipment-specific exemption or clearance. Where NPB does apply, registration and marking/PIB duties must be followed. [33/2025 original, PDF page 95 and Annex III B](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/395098/Permendag%2033%20Tahun%202025.pdf); [official status](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/332960/permendag-no-33-tahun-2025); [current NPB service](https://simpktn.kemendag.go.id/standalitu); [ministry FAQ](https://www.kemendag.go.id/faq).

## 7. Procurement brief for large supermarkets

Start with three **testable assortment hypotheses**:

1. A low-ticket six-unit spoon/fork replacement line, referencing TANICA DH shelf prices. Define each utensil's grade, thickness, finish and acceptance criteria before requesting a matching quotation.
2. An itemised family set. Compare knife-free and knife-inclusive variants separately; count components before assigning household size.
3. A compact portable kit. Show chopsticks and the case explicitly and explain whether the case is included in the headline count.

Request prices at buyer-selected quantities, MOQ by design/finish, replenishment lead time, barcode ownership, retail dimensions, carton quantity and defect handling. Public offers cannot establish a defensible MOQ or first-order volume. Calculate a trial from participating stores and agreed shelf allocation; reorder against actual sell-through, returns and lead time.

Store-only availability also means warehouse, online and individual-store stock should be separate procurement fields. A stock snapshot or review count is not a sales history and cannot demonstrate fast turnover.

## 8. Procurement brief for e-commerce companies

Use one contents list across title, attributes, images and packing sheet. State “one pair of chopsticks” and list the case separately. Require advance approval for brand substitutions: some observed Pocky and ET-1021 listings allow equivalent-brand substitutions, potentially unsuitable for a controlled brand programme.

Resolve the 18/10–SS430 conflict before comparative advertising. Photograph the actual delivered SKU; this article's generated illustration is not a product photo. Verify care and marking claims against documentation.

Compare **contribution per successfully delivered retail unit**:

**Net sales revenue − attributable product/packaging cost − channel/payment fees − fulfilment/delivery cost − expected return/defect cost.**

Use actual fee schedules, measured parcels, tax treatment and trial results. Observed retail prices are not ex-factory quotations or evidence of a target margin. Keep an offer only after the trial demonstrates both delivery economics and repeat-order supply.
