For Philippine supermarket and e-commerce buying teams, **the useful comparison is the complete set delivered to the customer, with its itemised contents and packaging**. A PHP179 twelve-piece set can serve four people with a fork, spoon and teaspoon each. A PHP199 twelve-piece offer can instead contain six spoon-and-fork pairs. The two offers solve different buying needs, despite similar prices and identical total counts.

The product, trade and statistical observations in this desk study were collected on **7 November 2026, Asia/Shanghai**; the Trustmark implementation evidence was updated on **17 November 2026**. The listed prices remain dated observations, not refreshed quotations. It includes 15 usable product observations from SM Home and IKEA Philippines, their own online channels, official statistics and regulatory texts. No store visit, purchase, consumer survey or laboratory test was conducted. Retail observations are in **Philippine pesos (PHP)**; trade values are current USD. The cover is an **AI-generated editorial illustration**, not a real SKU, factory photograph or packaging test.

Use the revised series when comparing years; do not treat an earlier publication's differently scoped e-commerce share as a directly comparable base. [PSA technical notes](https://psa.gov.ph/statistics/digital-economy/technical-notes).

These indicators justify evaluating physical and digital channels separately. They do not provide the sales velocity, conversion rate or inventory turns needed for a flatware purchase order.

## 4. Two packaging specifications, two different cost tests

IKEA365+ and MARTORP each provide six settings, but MARTORP adds cake forks. Their rectangular product-package envelopes are **1,840cm³ and 4,250cm³**, respectively: about **2.31 times** the volume for MARTORP, against weights of **1.37kg and 1.49kg**. This calculation uses the linked dimensions; it is not a courier quote or proof of shipping damage.

A supermarket buyer should request the retail-box footprint, facing width, barcode position, inner-pack quantity and distribution-centre replenishment unit. A larger presentation box must earn its shelf space through measured sales and margin. Do not use a consumer gift box as the specification for a store-delivery master carton.

An e-commerce seller should request the final parcel after restraints, edge protection and cushioning are added. Obtain quotations for the intended delivery destinations and service levels; use the selected carrier's actual volumetric-weight rule. The product-package weight alone is insufficient. For a portable kit, photograph the included case and explain any random-colour policy before the buyer orders.

Both channels need a repeatable counting process. A packed set with twelve pieces can still be wrong if one fork replaces a spoon. A practical supplier check records the number of each utensil, approved finish and packaging version against the purchase-order SKU, with a batch identifier for complaints.

## 5. Regulatory evidence: separate product safety, labels and online selling

**Consumer labels.** Republic Act7394, Article77, specifies consumer-package information including trade/business identity, the relevant Philippine manufacturer/importer/repacker address, general make, quantity and imported country of manufacture. Article81 addresses displayed retail prices. For a cutlery brief, give the local importer the actual composition and count for artwork approval; avoid treating “food grade” as a substitute for the label. [Consumer Act, WIPO's full-text reproduction](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/9574).

**Origin marking.** CMTA, Republic Act10863, Section710, requires conspicuous, legible and durable country-of-origin marking, subject to its exceptions and container provisions. A container-only solution is not automatically available for every product. Have the importer approve the mark and placement for the actual goods and packaging before shipment. [Supreme Court E-Library original](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/68014). CAO02-2019 was located, but its complete operational text was not retrieved through the attempted access paths; no additional procedural exemption is asserted.

**Food-contact use and standards.** The DOH rules for food establishments, Section3(h), require food-contact surfaces to be non-toxic, corrosion-resistant, cleanable and durable. That is a rule for covered establishments' equipment and utensils, not an automatic import approval for every retail flatware set. [DOH implementing rules in the Supreme Court E-Library](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/39219).

BPS lists **PNS ISO8442-2:2012** for stainless-steel and silver-plated cutlery. The catalogue is not the complete test text, and listing a standard does not by itself establish mandatory certification. Ordinary steel flatware was not identified by name in the mandatory-product list reviewed; absence from that list is not a shipment-specific exemption. [BPS standards catalogue](https://bps.dti.gov.ph/component/booklibrary/283/showCategory/621/materials-and-articles-in-contact-with-foodstuffs?Itemid=283), [BPS mandatory certification list](https://bps.dti.gov.ph/product-certification/list-of-products-under-mandatory-certification).

**Internet transactions.** The filed JAO24-03/2024 implementing rules cover specified B2B and B2C internet transactions. Section34 ties delivered type, quantity, quality and described packaging/accessories to the offer; it also addresses Filipino and/or English packaging information, merchant identity, invoices and complaints. Build a controlled listing from the approved sample, rather than copying unsupported competitor claims. [Official ONAR record](https://naro.law.upd.edu.ph/issuances/4857), [original PDF, pages14–16](https://naro.law.upd.edu.ph/storage/01JW4THMY8H7ZP3C25Z3JWSZB8.pdf).

**Trustmark: use DTI's later implementation announcements.** DAO25-12, issued **4 September 2025**, introduced mandatory-registration wording. DTI's own **16 October 2025** release records its 15 October decision to defer mandatory registration and continue voluntary applications. Its subsequent **22 December 2025** official statement extends voluntary registration **through 31 December 2026**. The specific voluntary-application answer in the current FAQ is consistent with that later announced implementation, although the FAQ introduction retains older mandatory wording. [DAO25-12, sections1–2 and7](https://trustmark.dti.gov.ph/img/%5BCTC%5D%2BDAO%2BNo.%2B25-12.pdf), [DTI deferral announcement](https://www.dti.gov.ph/dti-archives/dti-news-archived/dti-defers-mandatory-trustmark-registration-extends-voluntary-phase-until-year-end), [DTI extension statement](https://www.dti.gov.ph/dti-archives/dti-archived-press-statements-archived/dti-dti-extends-voluntary-trustmark-registration-online-businesses-until-december-2026), [DTI FAQ](https://trustmark.dti.gov.ph/faqs).

This is an account of **DTI's published implementation position**, not a claim that DAO25-12 has been formally repealed, that sellers have a permanent exemption or that mandatory registration automatically begins on 1 January 2027. The October release expressly maintains enforcement of other e-commerce laws. Before launch or a year-end policy change, the local selling entity should check DTI's latest implementation notice and its platform's requirements, while retaining its ordinary business, consumer-protection and product-compliance records. Trustmark participation does not certify a particular flatware SKU or replace its required evidence.

For tariff work, the Tariff Commission's current finder uses **AHTN2022** and 2024–2028 schedules. Select the national line, year, origin and preference conditions for the actual set; the HS8215 research aggregate cannot determine its duty. No shipment-specific rate, preferential origin eligibility, VAT treatment or complete permit clearance is established in this study. [Official finder guidance](https://newtc.tariffcommission.gov.ph/philippine-tariff-finder/).

## 6. Buying briefs for the two audiences

These are recommendations inferred from the observed offers, not best-seller findings or promised resale prices.

| Decision | Large supermarket buying team | E-commerce sales company |
|---|---|---|
| Opening assortment | Test a clearly counted spoon/fork-pair offer and an itemised family set as separate shelf propositions | Separate pair packs, family settings and portable kits into clearly described listings |
| Price reference | PHP179–199 is an observed entry-set band; PHP799–899 represents the two sampled Eurochef16 offers | Compare the delivered basket, including shipping and seller-funded promotions, not just the headline price |
| Upgrade logic | Require evidence for alloy/finish claims and show the actual added utensils or packaging value | Use accurate close-ups and contents diagrams; avoid implying an 18/10 or PVD claim proves food-contact compliance |
| Packaging | Approve shelf footprint, barcode, retail box, inner/master quantities and store-delivery protection | Approve packed-parcel size/weight, restraint against rubbing, count verification and dispatch photos |
| Replenishment | Track store-level units sold, weeks of stock, missing pieces and returns before adding facings | Track conversion, net contribution and return reasons by variant and destination before widening the range |

For an initial order, obtain quantity-tier quotations and samples before committing to a volume. The public evidence supplies no defensible factory MOQ, manufacturing lead time, credit term, retailer listing fee or reorder quantity.

## 7. Calculate return exposure without inventing a return rate

Use a contribution worksheet with **net sales receipts less landed merchandise, packaging, channel/payment fees, fulfilment, promotion funding and return-related cost**. For stores, include distribution-centre and shelf-handling costs. For online orders, include pick/pack, outbound delivery and the actual commercial or legal remedy obligations for the transaction.

Estimate expected return-related cost as the sum, across observed failure categories, of **event probability × net cost per event**. Missing utensils, wrong variants, transit abrasion and customer-preference returns require different remedies. Credit recoverable stock or supplier reimbursement only where supported. Do not assign a Philippine market return percentage from an unrepresentative product review or assume another retailer's return policy is your own.

Ask suppliers for a component-level material declaration, dimensions and mass, coating specification where relevant, sample and test evidence matched to the intended use, retail artwork, packing list, batch traceability, origin documents, replenishment options and written treatment of shortages. Knife blades may require a different material specification from spoons and forks; a single vague alloy claim for the whole set is insufficient.

The remaining commercial gaps are store-confirmed prices/availability, independently verified marketplace offers, actual carrier and platform charges, complete shipping-pack specifications and shipment-specific regulatory decisions. The immediate buying proposal is a **small, measurable assortment trial with explicit contents and separate retail/parcel packaging**, followed by replenishment based on the buyer's own records. Recheck the announced Trustmark implementation period before launch or year-end; keep that business-level decision separate from the evidence for the actual utensils.
