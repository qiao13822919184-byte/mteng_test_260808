For Vietnamese supermarket and e-commerce buying teams, a useful first decision is **which utensil is being replenished and what one customer order actually contains**. A two-spoon pack, a single appetizer spoon and a knife–fork–spoon combination have different shelf roles, freight economics and product-page requirements. A large family set should not automatically become the starting assortment.

This desk study was researched on **7 November 2026, Asia/Shanghai**. It combines 15 public product observations from two retail chains and their own online channels, national statistics, UN trade queries and original regulatory texts. It is not a store visit, purchase, consumer survey or laboratory test. Prices are **Vietnamese dong (VND)**; trade values are current USD. The cover is an **AI-generated editorial illustration** of generic stainless steel utensils and packaging, not a factory photograph, tested package or actual SKU.

## 1. Retail activity supports investigation, not a cutlery sales forecast

Vietnam's National Statistics Office estimated **2025 retail sales of consumer goods and services at VND 7,008.9 trillion**, up **9.2%** in current prices and **6.7%** after excluding price increases. This measure includes services: it is not merchandise-only turnover and cannot be assigned to flatware. Source: [NSO, fourth-quarter and full-year 2025 report](https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/data-and-statistics/2026/01/socio-economic-situation-in-the-fourth-quarter-and-2025/).

For online retail, the Ministry of Industry and Trade's e-commerce agency reported **more than USD25 billion in 2024**, growth of **more than 20%**, and approximately **9%** of retail goods and consumer-services revenue. These are the agency's stated estimates and denominator, not marketplace-only GMV or cutlery revenue. Source: [iDEA's September 2025 account of its Vietnam International Sourcing conference](https://idea.gov.vn/default.aspx?do=detail&id=e7b244fe-217b-4b96-be3c-b1d1c725765c&page=news).

The two statistics refer to different years and measures. Do not divide the 2024 dollar figure by 2025 dong sales or infer a category growth rate. A newer 2025 domestic-market report was located, but its full download was inaccessible during this audit; its headline estimate is not substituted into a continuous series here. The verified context justifies examining both channels. It does not establish household replacement frequency, demand by province or an opening order quantity.

## 2. What the three-year import check can—and cannot—show

The query used Vietnam as reporter (704), imports, annual frequency, world and China partners, with **HS8215** and **HS821191** checked separately. Returned 2023 records use **H6 / HS2022**. HS8215 covers multiple base-metal table and kitchen articles and sets; it does not isolate stainless steel. HS821191 covers fixed-blade table knives. Actual sets require classification on their contents, so the two headings are not a ready-made retail-market total.

The 2023 rows are marked aggregate and not directly reported at that level. The world HS8215 row has no usable net weight; the China row's approximately 297,337 kg is **estimated**. The knife weights are estimated too. These fields cannot support a trustworthy price per utensil, and an unavailable quantity is not zero physical demand.

China accounts for approximately **79.87% of the returned 2023 HS8215 value**, calculated from unrounded values. That is a historical sourcing signal within this broad heading, not current supplier share for stainless flatware. No 2023–2025 CAGR is calculated. A buyer needing a current country estimate should obtain matching Vietnam customs detail and, if necessary, a separately labelled partner-export mirror series. FOB exports, CIF imports, timing differences and estimated weights must remain visible rather than being joined into one apparently complete trend.

## 4. Supermarket procurement: develop a replenishment assortment

A defensible test assortment would separate **eating spoons, short coffee spoons, soup spoons and forks by function**, with a small combination offer tested separately. This is an editorial inference from the observed formats, not a recommendation to copy the sampled brands or their designs.

For confirmed two-piece packs, Co.op's 829B offer implies **VND14,250 per spoon**, while its large 304 smooth-handle pair implies **VND50,500 per spoon**. These are different designs and listings. The difference cannot be attributed to steel grade alone: dimensions, piece weight, finish, packaging, brand and promotion must be matched first. AEON's small-fork and short-coffee-spoon pairs each imply **VND36,800 per piece**, but a fork and a spoon still have different functions.

| Proposed supermarket decision | Evidence to request before ordering | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Replacement pair on a hook | Retail-unit barcode, exact two-piece contents, card dimensions and retention sample | Several offers use two-piece formats; title and checkout unit must agree |
| Single utensil beside replacement packs | Single-unit barcode, sleeve/card specification and replenishment case count | Tihome single offers create a separate unit-price reference |
| Small knife–fork–spoon combination | Component list, component-specific alloy and individual dimensions | One set-wide material description is insufficient for component approval |
| Store replenishment carton | Sellable units per inner/carton, carton dimensions and gross weight | None of the product pages establishes this transport specification |

Use actual approved samples to check the fork tines, bowl edges, handle transitions, finish consistency and packaging retention. Specify acceptance criteria with the buying team rather than presenting an invented national inspection standard. If a proposed carton contains **24 two-piece retail packs**, it contains **48 utensils**, but the store receives **24 saleable units**. Record both counts on the order and replenishment sheet. This is an illustrative packing calculation, not a sampled retailer's carton specification.

Before rollout, compare sell-through and out-of-stock days by store and function. A low-priced spoon pair cannot justify the same opening quantity for forks or combinations. Obtain branch-level sales or a controlled trial to turn the assortment hypothesis into an order.

## 5. E-commerce procurement: sell a clear pack and cost the shipped order

An e-commerce seller needs a separate customer-facing unit definition. State whether the price buys one utensil, one two-piece pack or one complete combination. Show all included pieces together and repeat the count in the title, specification and dispatch check. A photograph of two pieces beside an ambiguous “item” unit can cause a fulfilment dispute even if the metal itself meets specification.

The AEON pages offer useful specification benchmarks: the small fork and short coffee spoon both list **140 mm**, but **19 g** and **24 g** per piece respectively. The long coffee spoon lists **190 mm / 30 g**, while the large eating spoon lists **195 mm / 48 g**. A near-identical length therefore does not establish equivalent weight or function. Use supplier-verified dimensions and tolerances for your own product; do not transfer these measurements to a different SKU.

Calculate the economics per shipped order:

**Revenue retained after discounts and applicable taxes − merchandise landed cost − platform/payment fees − picking and shipping packaging − delivery subsidy − expected returns/claims = order contribution.**

Use the actual fee schedule and packed dimensions for the chosen fulfilment route. A two-piece pack may reduce a customer's purchase commitment but leave less revenue to absorb a parcel charge. Test whether a clearly described multipack improves order contribution; do not assume that bundling automatically increases demand. The sampled pages do not provide platform fees, return rates or wholesale margins.

Keep preorders distinct from available stock in the supply plan. An expected online availability date is not a promise that an importer can deliver replenishment cartons to every province. Confirm destination coverage, dispatch date, return conditions and who pays for an incomplete or damaged pack. Maintain separate retail packaging and parcel-protection specifications, even when both channels use the same utensils.

## 6. Import, food contact and labels: update the document set

**Food-contact evidence.** [QCVN12-3:2011/BYT, original technical regulation](https://nifc.gov.vn/ajax/research/downloadfile?filecode=3ad711c9-9b58-4906-82e6-6f0913ee36a8), covers metal packaging and utensils intended for direct food contact. Ask a competent laboratory to map the actual alloy, coatings, intended use and applicable methods to the finished item. A “304” description or a retailer's absence-of-metals claim does not substitute for applicable test evidence. This study does not certify any sampled product.

**Declaration route and changing rules.** Articles 4–5 of [Decree15/2018/ND-CP](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2018/02/15.signed.pdf) establish the self-declaration framework for the listed food-related products, including food containers and direct-contact packaging, with exceptions. Where applicable, the dossier includes the declaration and a qualifying laboratory report issued within the preceding 12 months. Have the Vietnamese importer confirm the route and receiving authority for the exact utensil and its materials, together with import inspection requirements.

Do not assume the January 2026 replacement framework applies unchanged. [Resolution15/2026/NQ-CP](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2026/4/15-nqcp.signed.pdf), dated 6 April 2026, suspends Decree46/2026 and Resolution66.13/2026 until the amended Food Safety Law and its implementing decree take effect; during that suspension, Decree15/2018 and its implementing guidance continue. Check the operative framework again before shipment. This is a conditional suspension, not a permanent exemption.

**Labels.** Chapter IV of [Decree37/2026/ND-CP](https://congbaocdn.chinhphu.vn/180507251028987904/2026/2/10/37signed-1770718448911444649723.pdf) is the relevant 2026 text for the general labelling questions discussed here. Article39 addresses mandatory Vietnamese information and supplementary labels for imports; Article42 distinguishes labels at import clearance from information on goods circulating in Vietnam. Product name, responsible entity/address, origin and applicable category-specific information need review. Preserve the original label when adding required Vietnamese content. Article97 repeals the earlier general labelling decrees, while Article98 provides transition arrangements for qualifying existing goods and previously produced labels. Do not treat the transition as blanket permission to print a new old-format label.

**Online disclosure.** [E-commerce Law122/2025/QH15](https://congbaocdn.chinhphu.vn/180507251028987904/2026/1/27/122signed-17694826033991158478619.pdf), effective 1 July 2026, addresses transaction disclosures in Article11 and product-quality/mandatory-label information for direct sellers and marketplace sellers in Articles16 and21. Map the physical pack and product page to the applicable duties. Avoid copying outdated seller-compliance checklists or treating a generated photograph as product evidence.

**Customs cost remains SKU-specific.** This research does not confirm a national tariff line, duty, preferential origin entitlement or permit outcome for an actual shipment. Ask the importer/broker to classify the exact single item or set, document the current tariff and origin treatment, and confirm taxes, inspection and required records. Keep those responses with the commercial invoice, packing list and approved specification before agreeing a landed-cost target.

## 7. A purchase brief both channels can use

| Field to lock | Supermarket brief | E-commerce brief |
|---|---|---|
| Customer selling unit | Single, two-piece pack or itemised combination | Same count in title, options, images and parcel check |
| Product specification | Function, dimensions, weight, component alloy, finish and buyer tolerances | Identical physical specification plus usable size/weight disclosure |
| Packaging | Hook/shelf format, barcode, inner and carton counts | Retail pack plus measured shipping pack and protection |
| Compliance | Importer-confirmed route, applicable test evidence and approved Vietnamese label | Applicable pack information reflected online; clear transaction conditions |
| Reorder evidence | Store-level sell-through and replenishment time | Contribution per shipped order, claims and stock/dispatch performance |

Ask suppliers to quote the utensil, retail pack and transport pack separately, state minimum quantities for each packaging version, and confirm sample and replenishment lead times. No factory capability, minimum order or lead time is asserted here. Use [Yumingxing's contact page](https://www.mteng.ltd/contact) to discuss a documented specification and request confirmation for the particular proposal.

The procurement opportunity to test is a **clearly specified replenishment range with channel-specific packaging and fulfilment**, supported by real local price references. Unresolved trade years, branch availability, pack-unit ambiguity, exact customs treatment and shipment-specific documentation remain explicit checks. None should be hidden inside a national market-size estimate or an unsupported promise of compliance.
