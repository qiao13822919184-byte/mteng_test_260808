The receiving team may count shipping cartons while the purchase order lists sellable cutlery sets and individual pieces. A carton total alone cannot show whether the correct SKU and configuration arrived. Use a **receiving discrepancy record** to compare the delivered goods with the approved order and packing information. It records observations for the buyer's follow-up; it does not decide title transfer, insurance, liability or contractual acceptance.

Yumingxing's [buying process](/en/) asks parties to confirm carton marks, packing details and shipment arrangements. Its [RFQ guide](/en/blog/what-to-include-in-a-cutlery-rfq/) asks for quantity by piece and configuration. The [pieces, sets and cartons guide](/en/blog/flatware-pieces-sets-cartons-order-check/) explains the unit conversion before ordering. This article starts **after arrival**, when the receiving team must record what physically came in.

## Make one receiving row per SKU and pack configuration

| Field | Record at the receiving point |
|---|---|
| Order reference | Purchase order, approved SKU, revision and sellable unit definition. |
| Shipment reference | Packing-list version, carton mark and shipment identifier supplied for this delivery. |
| Count basis | Expected and observed shipping cartons; expected and observed sellable sets or pieces by SKU. |
| Carton condition | Visible damage or broken seals, identified by carton number or mark when possible. |
| Opened-unit check | Which cartons were opened, what configuration was found, and whether mixed packs were separated. |
| Evidence and owner | Dated photos, count sheet, receiving contact and person assigned to resolve each difference. |

First reconcile carton marks against the packing list, then check the agreed sellable-unit definition. If a carton is reported as containing complete sets, do not silently count loose pieces as sets. Keep a missing carton, a damaged carton and a wrong-SKU carton as different observations. When a difference is found, preserve its carton identity and send a clear, dated record to the supplier and the buyer's logistics contact under the agreed process.

The [pre-shipment inspection brief](/en/blog/cutlery-pre-shipment-inspection-brief/) concerns checks before dispatch; receiving is a separate event and may expose different issues. The [retail-box and shipping-carton brief](/en/blog/cutlery-retail-packaging-shipping-carton-brief/) helps distinguish a sellable unit from a transport carton. Do not erase the original count after a recount; add the corrected count, time and reason. The purchase contract and shipping terms determine the formal claim or acceptance procedure, so follow those documents for deadlines and remedies rather than treating this worksheet as legal advice.
