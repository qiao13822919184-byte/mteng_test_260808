A cutlery retail box presents the sellable product; a shipping carton groups and protects the packed goods for handling. Specify them separately. Record the contents, dimensions, weight, artwork and approval version at each level. An attractive gift box does not, by itself, demonstrate that the shipment is ready for its delivery route.

This guide is a purchasing worksheet for supermarket buyers, distributors and private-label sellers. The calculations below use hypothetical inputs, not Yumingxing packing specifications or freight quotations.

## Start with the sellable unit

Write down what the customer buys: an individual spoon, a place setting or a larger set. List the number of each component rather than relying on the phrase “one set.” State whether the unit includes a tray, sleeve, care card or other insert.

Then describe how the sellable units are grouped. An inner pack may be useful for the project, but it is not automatically required. If there is no inner pack, record that explicitly.

The [GS1 measurement standard](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/ppm/) distinguishes consumer and non-consumer packaging levels and defines measurement conventions. If a retailer requests GS1-based data, confirm the applicable level and method before supplying dimensions.

## Keep three levels visible in the brief

| Level | Information to define | Approval item |
|---|---|---|
| Retail unit | Piece composition, presentation, inserts, artwork and identification | Complete packed retail sample |
| Inner pack, if used | Retail units per inner pack, grouping and identification | Confirmed grouping specification |
| Shipping carton | Units per carton, external dimensions, weight and carton marks | Packed carton specification and agreed checks |

Keep the product variant attached to this record. Different finishes, set configurations or box designs may require separate packing entries. Do not assume one carton specification covers every option in the collection.

## Check units before comparing prices

Here is a hypothetical order: each retail set contains 4 pieces, each shipping carton contains 12 retail sets, and the order contains 80 cartons.

The order therefore contains **960 retail sets** and **3,840 individual pieces**:

`12 sets/carton × 80 cartons = 960 sets`

`4 pieces/set × 960 sets = 3,840 pieces`

A price per piece cannot be compared directly with a price per set. Ask which unit the quote uses and whether the specified packaging is included. These sample quantities do not describe an available MOQ or standard carton.

## Use carton volume carefully

For an illustrative rectangular carton measuring 40 × 30 × 25 cm externally, geometric volume is:

`0.40 m × 0.30 m × 0.25 m = 0.030 m³`

For 80 identical cartons, the sum is `2.40 m³` before considering pallets, spacing or other handling requirements. This is a volume calculation, not a freight bill, chargeable-weight calculation or guarantee of container utilization. Actual shipping arrangements need the packed dimensions, weight and carrier requirements.

Label dimensions with units and an agreed orientation. Separate external packed dimensions from an internal tray opening or a flat artwork size; they answer different questions.

## Approve appearance and protection separately

Approve artwork version, color reference, product description and any supplied barcode data. Have the responsible brand or retailer verify the required label content. Do not copy another seller’s artwork or assume a box that works for one platform meets every channel’s current requirements.

Also agree how the pieces are held and separated, how the carton is packed and what checks are required for the intended route. If transport testing is requested, define the method, sample configuration and acceptance criteria with the relevant specialist. This worksheet does not claim that a particular pack has passed a test.

## Common questions

### Is a gift box also a shipping box?

It may form part of a packing system, but its suitability for shipment must be evaluated for that system and route. Presentation alone does not establish protective performance.

### Can one carton specification cover mixed finishes?

Only if the agreed packed configuration actually supports it. Identify the quantity of each variant and confirm how the contents can be checked when the carton is received.
